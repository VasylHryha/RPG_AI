1. **HIGH — The claimed power does not support 60 clusters; joint endpoint power is missing.** Proposal §4, lines 56–58.

   A design effect of 2 permits perfectly concordant orientations: each cluster is one Bernoulli observation. At one-sided alpha 0.005, an exact binomial test with 50 clusters rejects at 35 or more successes and has power **0.56918** at true p = 0.70. With 60 it rejects at 41 or more and has power **0.66916**. Thus “about 50 clusters already give power above 0.95” is false under the stated allowance. A normal planning calculation allowing this dependence gives about 105 clusters before finite-sample/discreteness adjustments, not 50. This is a counterexample to the stated justification, not a substitute power analysis for the unspecified bootstrap.

   From the committed 20 regular validation clusters, win fractions have mean 0.8 and sample SD **0.3402785**; cluster S has mean **4.675** and sample SD **8.1277222**. The draft never supplies those spreads or a reproducible calculation. More importantly, E1 requires BOTH win and S bounds to pass. A true win probability of 0.70 alone does not specify mean S or the joint distribution; it can coexist with negative mean S. Power for the win condition alone cannot justify endpoint power, and marginal 95% powers do not generally imply 95% joint power.

   **Fix:** freeze a bounded sizing rule, desired joint power, planning alternatives for win and S, dependence/variance assumptions and uncertainty allowance. Evaluate the exact chosen decision algorithm using joint cluster observations or explicitly conservative assumptions. Publish a reproducible planning receipt with both heads, floors/cap, n chosen and fight/time projections, before drawing judging entropy. Retain DESIGN §10's above-2,000-cluster owner trade-off stop, or explicitly propose its replacement. Treat 60 as provisional unless this computation actually justifies it; no post-judging adjustment.

2. **HIGH — “Cluster bootstrap” does not identify a test or establish the advertised alpha.** Proposal §4, lines 54–55.

   Cluster resampling is the correct dependence unit. But percentile, basic, BCa and studentized bounds differ; the method, quantile convention, actual RNG seed/algorithm, undefined/zero-variance handling and assumptions are missing. Increasing resamples to 100,000 reduces Monte Carlo error, not inferential calibration error. Official [SciPy bootstrap documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.bootstrap.html) distinguishes the methods and warns that BCa bounds can be undefined on degenerate data. This is documentation evidence, not a requirement to use SciPy.

   A concrete failure of a naive percentile/basic implementation: let independent clusters contain two concordant fights, with (win fraction, S) = (1, +1) with probability 50/51 and (0, −50) with probability 1/51. These are within the registered score range. Mean S is exactly zero, so the endpoint is null despite its high win rate. With 60 clusters, the probability that every observation is (1, +1) is **(50/51)^60 = 0.3047823**. A percentile/basic interval that collapses to the constant observed mean supports both components on that event. This is an admissible metric-range counterexample, not evidence that this simulator has that distribution. It shows why a generic bootstrap cannot promise distribution-free alpha 0.005. Merely choosing BCa does not solve rare unobserved losses.

   **Fix:** specify and pin the full algorithm, paired resampling of cluster win/S data, strict comparisons and degeneracy/failure behavior. Justify calibration under declared assumptions, including skew, rare adverse scores and the novice all-win boundary, or choose a valid conservative method for bounded cluster means. If the claim is only approximate nominal control, say so and disclose its limits; do not advertise guaranteed familywise control. Any development calibration must be independently authorized and use no judging entropy. Predeclare the response to invalid bounds; do not select a fallback after observing the panel.

   **What is sound:** for each head the null is win rate ≤ 0.5 OR mean S ≤ 0. Requiring both component tests at 0.005 is an intersection-union test and needs no additional split. Bonferroni over E1/E2 gives familywise 0.01 for their support claims **if** the component tests are valid. Correlation between heads or components does not invalidate that logic.

3. **HIGH — Failure-as-loss and stop-on-any-failure are conflicting contracts; S remains undefined.** Proposal §§3, 5 and 6, lines 37, 68 and 77.

   The draft promises every controller/numerical/native failure contributes a loss to primary analysis, but also stops the entire run on any failure. Later scheduled cells then have no outcomes. W = 0 for the failed fight does not define S for a malformed/non-finite result, nor say how its paired cluster is formed. Averaging surviving rows would violate the cluster denominator. A descriptive comparator failure could also prevent completion of both primary panels. The existing v7c protocol rejects failed rows; it is not already an implementation of the proposed loss policy.

   **Fix:** distinguish individually attributable controller/numerical failures, infrastructure failures and integrity/identity failures. Define their detection predicates, affected batch/cells, retry prohibition and endpoint dependencies. For attributable failures, keep W = 0 and a frozen full denominator; explicitly define an owner-approved adverse S score (and label its estimand as failure-coded), or mark the S condition unevaluable and prevent support. For global interruption, report PARTIAL and endpoint coverage/reasons; do not turn unattempted cells into an ordinary completed panel. Specify whether a recoverable per-fight failure allows the remaining sealed schedule to continue. Descriptive-arm failures should have a declared effect rather than silently alter primary inference. Include finite/count/time checks and both sides' failure counters.
