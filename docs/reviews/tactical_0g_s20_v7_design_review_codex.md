## R2 — High: forced commitment does not reproduce P12's gun geometry

C1 changes a preferred distance for an own-gun/enemy-gun pair. P5/P11 instead choose one movement focus: the weakest reachable enemy gun for that gun, or a shared weakest reachable-by-any-gun anchor, falling back to the weakest living gun. They construct a radial post and replace the **entire** command (`native/s4_focus_probe_v1.cpp:13–28`, `astelia_cpp/s4_spacing_probe_v1/spacing.cpp:13–39`). The movement focus can differ from the firing target when no gun is reachable.

V6 uses a weighted sum over its capped enemy set, independent of the firing-target choice (`native/s4_v6_controller.cpp:65–79`, `native/s3_controller.cpp:91–119`). Its final goal is 200 px along the resulting velocity, with multiplier min(1, speed). Neither the single focus nor P5's goal/multiplier follows from substituting R−12 into this sum. Check 1 exempts neighbour and spacing terms, but not this enemy-force/actuator difference.

**Counterexample, from the formulas only:** an own gun at (500,350), with range 320 and minRange 0, faces otherwise equal enemy guns at (180,350) and (820,350), with no own neighbours. With c=1 and equal weights, the two R−12 restoring terms cancel. V6's construction holds. P5 chooses one gun by HP/id and commands (488,350) or (512,350), moving at full multiplier. No exempted neighbour or spacing term exists in this example.

**Fix:** declare the gun movement-focus selector, shared-anchor acquisition, firing-target relationship, all fallback cases, and the exact action construction. Choose either a witness-compatible radial-post command or the retained force-sum law; the latter is a new geometry hypothesis and requires replacing check 1 with its actual invariant. Include symmetric enemies, no reachable gun, target death, no enemy battery and coincidence fixtures. Compare all action fields, not just preferred distances.

**C1b focus mismatch (high):** adding `(1−hp/maxhp)` only to legal enemy-gun targets is precisely computable. It does not implement P4/P12's weakest-reachable-gun priority. V6 alignment spans [−1,1], and engagement contributes up to 1, so a soft target can outscore a wounded gun. Even with equal baseline scores, a healthier incumbent gun survives when the HP-bonus gain is less than 0.2 (`native/s4_v6_controller.cpp:16,70–77`). The inherited retention test is a one-sided improvement margin, not a symmetric “±0.2” rule. **Fix:** either adopt the exact witnessed gun-first/lowest-HP ordering and its fallback, or label the soft score as a new hypothesis and remove focus-equivalence language. Define actual-target publication into target-group memory, and test legal soft targets, competing guns, the exact retention boundary and no reachable gun. The fixed roster's equal gun maxhp does preserve fractional-versus-absolute HP ordering among guns; baseline score and retention remain the problem even there.

## R3 — High: the escort gate does not identify its controlling pair

V3 modes exist only for out-ranged/equal-range pairs; an out-ranging pair returns the continuous v2 kite law before creating a mode (`native/s3_controller.cpp:50–57`). V6 updates every living eligible pair before computing movement (`native/s4_v6_controller.cpp:68`). “A ranged unit in commit mode toward a gun-threatening enemy ranged unit” does not identify whether C3 uses its firing target, the nearest enemy to the ranged unit, the nearest enemy to the assigned gun, any committed threat, or every relevant pair.

P12's direction selector is specifically the nearest enemy ranged **within 400 px of the assigned own gun**, with no recent-damage or native-reach requirement (`astelia_cpp/s4_escort_probe_v1/escort.cpp:20–29`). An enemy at 350 px qualifies as this direction source but is outside native direct reach of that gun in the admitted roster. “Gun-threatening” could instead mean v2Threat (positive outgoing EMA and native reach), or P14's geometry-only threat to any gun. These are three different predicates. P12 also escorts when no such enemy exists, using enemy-gun centroid/nearest-gun fallbacks; those branches provide no enemy-ranged pair to gate.

**Fix:** name one predicate and one gate reference, its selection order, treatment of absent/kite pairs, and gate behaviour for every P12 fallback. Specify whether the escort command replaces or adds to the enemy-motion and neighbour terms, its final multiplier/stop, and its precedence over escape forces. Use observed current gun centres, as P12 does. A per-unit mode would be another declared design change, not unchanged v3 hysteresis.

**Pair-memory qualification:** all modes for one own unit share the same c, so existing pairs switch together outside the deadband. They need not share history inside it: an older escaping pair can coexist with a newly initialized committed pair at c=0. Test that case, threat reassignment, assigned-gun death, equal-range ranged pairs, missing threats and centroid fallback. The proposed c=0 check must distinguish existing modes staying put from new modes initializing.

## R4 — High: the proposed comparison cannot isolate resonator timing

P12 replaces gun/ranged movement; v7 retains v6 neighbour and enemy-motion terms, softens focus targeting and adds P15 ranged spacing. P12 has no P15 shift. These differences persist with all units forced to commit. In addition, P5/P11/P12 override action targets **after** v6 has computed and stored its baseline targets; target-group coupling still uses that internal history (`native/s4_v6_controller.cpp:86`, `astelia_cpp/s4_focus_probe_v1/POLICY.md:5–7`). An integrated v7 score would ordinarily publish its modified target, changing subsequent K_t groups. Frozen comparators and paired seeds do not remove these confounds; tuning v7 while freezing comparator knobs measures package improvement under unequal optimization histories.

**Fix:** retain P12 as a descriptive performance witness, but add a matched v7 action-mode intervention if the question remains “does timing add?”. Run the same selected v7 knobs and code with the commit/escape gate forced always-commit, keeping geometry, spacing, score, neighbour terms and target-memory semantics identical. Declare how continuous kite commitment is handled. This tests the gate's contribution conditional on that package; oscillator-dependent similarity/targeting still exist in both arms. A separate zero-ω intervention is needed for a rotation-contribution claim, and further coupling interventions for a synchrony claim. Select knobs without using any validation-arm outcomes. Alternatively narrow the question to performance of the whole revised controller versus frozen P12 and withdraw causal timing language.

Section 20's statement that an equal result at μ≪0, ω→0 means oscillation adds nothing (`:1298`) conflicts with §18.1 F6. Selected parameters and equal counts on a small panel neither prove equivalence nor necessity. Report them as optimizer diagnostics; any intervention result must be conditional on its declared setting and uncertainty.

## R5 — Medium: spacing is not a separation guarantee, and its action semantics are unfinished

C2 specifies adding shifts in every mode, but not the base goal, final clip/order, or multiplier after the shift. V6 can publish multiplier 0 after force cancellation; adding a goal shift then produces no commanded movement unless the multiplier is recomputed. Exact P11 **preserves** its pre-shift multiplier, even when zero (`spacing.cpp:28–37`), while P15 recomputes it from the final clipped-goal error (`astelia_cpp/s4_escort_probe_v2/escort.cpp:25–33`). Those inherited conventions are different.

The witness already discloses this limitation: P11 regular has 239 zero-multiplier/nonzero-shift ticks and 31,811/152,506 raw goals outside the focus band (`astelia_cpp/S4_SPACING_PROBE.md:22,28,36`). A threshold S is a soft simultaneous repulsion scale; it does not enforce minimum distance. V6's ~56 px figure is an isolated in-phase pair equilibrium. Its neighbours include all own roles, at most eight within 300 px, and external forces shift equilibria (`native/s4_v6_complex.cpp:41–48`, `native/s4_v6_controller.cpp:62–64`). It is not an existing same-role spacing guarantee.

**Fix:** write the full action order for each role/mode: base command → snapshot/id-ordered shift → clipping → declared multiplier/stop. Preserve or change P11's zero-multiplier convention explicitly. State the exact coincidence convention (lower id −x, higher id +x), no-enemy/no-battery behaviour, and whether melee is excluded. Test cancellation, crowded/coincident neighbours, boundaries, zero base movement and both escape/commit modes. Replace “spacing holds” with the claimed soft rule and report achieved spacing and shell multiplicity. P15's 6/20 versus P12's 7/20 does not establish a benefit from ranged spacing; its incorporation is a disclosed new hypothesis.

## R6 — Medium: minimum range and the 12 px margin require an explicit contract

The witness post is **max(minRange, range−12)**, not bare R−12 (`native/s4_focus_probe_v1.cpp:24`). Section 20 removes that guard and does not restrict admission to minRange≤R−12. The current fixed game roster has minRange 0, so this is not evidence that its historical fights violated minimum range. Nevertheless the general observation-facing rule is incomplete for narrow or invalid gun bands.

Spacing can consume the 12 px outer margin or pull a shifted post inward; native clipping and enemy motion add further deviations. For example, a 20 px outward radial shift takes a nominal 308 px post to 328 px, outside 320 px reach. Exact P11 deliberately permits this; silently projecting back into range would change its witnessed intervention. P15 likewise moves many goals inside the assigned gun's 49 px splash bound.

**Fix:** retain the witness minRange guard or declare a different bound with an admitted-domain rule and failure/fallback for impossible bands. State whether final goals may leave the legal annulus; if they may, call 12 px a nominal post margin, not a shot-range guarantee. If annulus projection is chosen, disclose and validate the new geometry. Audit raw and clipped goal distances, actual in-band time, spacing, escort-to-gun clearances and movement toward the chosen post. Keep 12 px explicitly witness-derived rather than catalog-derived.

## R7 — High: “adds anything”, ≥P12 and the sample-size interpretation are inconsistent

The validation panel gives 20 fights per arm/head from **10 paired clusters**, a tenfold reduction from v6's 100-cluster validation. Section 20 declares no reason for that reduction or uncertainty protocol. Equality with P12 is an observed count match, not progress, superiority or statistical noninferiority. It passes even at 0=0. An exploratory 11/20 meets the observed >50% count condition, but does not establish a population win rate above 50%. The earlier descriptive probes correctly avoid that inference.

**Fix:** retain a small panel only with an explicit descriptive-development boundary. For each cluster average its two binary outcomes, then report paired v7−comparator differences, discordant outcomes and uncertainty using clusters as the resampling/sampling unit. Specify any inference method and margin before outcomes, or state that no inferential verdict is made. Separate “observed witness match” (≥), “observed improvement” (>), and the full owner gate; add a positive witness/floor condition so 0=0 cannot be progress. A population superiority, equivalence or >50% claim needs a separately approved sample-size/power and decision protocol. Do not enlarge or rerun a completed panel in response to its counts.
