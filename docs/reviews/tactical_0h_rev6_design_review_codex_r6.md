## Numbered new findings

### 1. R6-1 — LOW, nonblocking: scope the terminal-outcome list so resource refusals retain their own codes

**Evidence:** R6:653–659 usefully gives the six B-path trial conditions stable names, and R6:664–668 distinguishes requests, candidate attempts and accepted births. However, the adjacent “Terminal outcomes” list at R6:661 contains only `accepted`, `exhausted`, `no_output` and `no_root`. The common birth contract still requires `cap` and `cost` refusals (R6:70; base:158), while G0' expressly excludes those resource refusals and counts terminal placement/search failures instead (R6:399–407,668).

This does **not** justify removing resource checks or changing G0'. The surviving clauses already determine the intended behavior. The small gap is whether R6:661 is an exhaustive birth-outcome enum or only the frontier-search outcome list. A cost-denied feasible candidate must not become `exhausted` merely because that enum has no resource entry; doing so would change G0' accounting.

**Fix:** label the list as frontier-search outcomes, with resource dispositions recorded separately, or include `cap` and `cost` in the terminal birth schema. Keep B1/B-out `placement` and B-path geometric `exhausted` distinct from resource refusals. State how an opportunity left unprocessed after the per-check birth quota is reached is recorded; it is not a placement failure. Retain the existing G0' rule and one terminal record per processed request, with per-candidate failures confined to attempt diagnostics. This is a logging clarification, not another endpoint or a capacity-reservation requirement.

### 2. R6-2 — LOW, nonblocking: F6 needs an undefined-result rule for the within-episode mean direction

**Evidence:** F6 correlates the last visible encoded angle with the mean hidden-interval output angle (R6:543). Its null rule covers zero circular variance in the episode-level variables and fewer than five episodes with an output (R6:544), but not an undefined **within-episode** circular mean. Output presence alone does not guarantee that mean exists.

An analytic example is a free output with frozen rate `omega_O = pi + pi/6`, within the permitted rate range (base:122). After carrier demodulation its angle advances by `pi/6` rad/s. Over the 12 s hidden interval, 120 evenly spaced decisions traverse one complete circle, whose mean phasor is zero. Its mean direction is undefined even though O exists at every decision. Floating-point residue can supply an arbitrary angle if `arg` is taken without a resultant check. This is a mathematical counterexample, not an executed fixture or a claim about the actual grown medium.

**Fix:** specify the within-episode summary as the circular mean of decoded, carrier-demodulated angles `beta`, record its resultant, and preregister its undefined/numerically degenerate-resultant disposition. Report such episodes as lacking a defined mean direction; count finite paired angles, rather than output presence alone, for the minimum-five rule. If the correlation cannot be formed, return null with the reason. Keep raw angle/resultant traces and F6 descriptive. Do not turn this into a memory PASS requirement or substitute a new summary after seeing results.
