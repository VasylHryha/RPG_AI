Ranged units retain v6's multiplier, `min(1, speed)`, or 0 when its computed vector is below 1e-9 (`src/native/s4_v6_controller.cpp:59–80`). There is no ranged P5 preferred radius or focus to transplant. Keeping the original ranged multiplier can leave an escort far from its point with multiplier 0; commanding movement toward that point can move it on the same observation. Those are different arms, not merely two implementations of one specified arm. **Fix:** the drafter must explicitly choose whether P12/P13 retain the prepared v6 ranged multiplier, or recompute movement from escort-point error. For the latter, state the exact predicate/tolerance, whether error uses the raw or clipped point, and stop distance. Keep gun decisions exactly P11 and targeting exactly the prepared v6 decision. Do not copy a gun's multiplier or silently substitute full-speed movement. A goal-only reading is plausible, but the reference to P5 also plausibly requests its active-commit convention; the text must choose.

2. **Medium / blocking for a total policy — direction has undefined and unordered cases.** The assignment to a living own gun, including own-gun id ties, and the no-enemy-ranged-within-400 fallback to living enemy-gun centroid are computable from `Observation`. Dead units are already absent in the native bridge; explicit hp>0 filtering remains appropriate for fixtures. But a coincident chosen enemy ranged unit gives a zero vector. The centroid can also coincide with the assigned gun even when every enemy gun is elsewhere: own gun at (500,400), enemy guns at (400,400) and (600,400), no enemy ranged in the cutoff. Normalizing either zero vector is undefined and the bridge then converts nonfinite decisions to controller failures/hold. Equal-distance enemy ranged units have no tie rule; “within 400” also needs its boundary stated. **Fix:** the drafter declares a deterministic observation-only zero-direction fallback, enemy-ranged ties by a stated ordering, inclusive/exclusive cutoff, and fixed-order centroid summation from the same prepare snapshot. Do not invent remembered directions, RNG, world state or an additional target priority. With no living own gun or no living enemy gun, use the complete P11/v6 fallthrough, not a stale escort assignment. The chosen fallback is part of the intervention and must precede combat.
