**The active test is not a bootstrap or a win/S intersection-union test.** It is one one-sided bounded-mean betting test per head at alpha=0.005. The estimates and clipping now match the stated construction; final-time positive capital and log(1/alpha) are declared one-sided choices rather than the recommended two-sided running intersection. My direct validity check is that each factor is at least 1-c=1/2 and, under conditional mean at most m, has conditional expectation at most 1. Consequently the capital is a nonnegative test supermartingale and the final rejection probability is at most alpha. For a fixed predictable base bet, both its unclipped and clipped factors are nonincreasing in m and agree at the clipping boundary. Conservative grid inversion is therefore sound.
