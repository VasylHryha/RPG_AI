## R2-F1 — High: specify P16 at the selected v7 knobs, separately from historical P16

`DESIGN_0G.md:1482` calls P16 the same code with the gate fixed. Lines 1483 and 1509 explicitly put v6 at the tuned v7 knobs, but do not explicitly do so for P16. Check 7 also asks for delivered-version identity, while the earlier §20 comparator paragraph describes frozen historical P12/v6/morale comparators. Historical P16 knobs are separately fixed in its v3/v4 policies. The revision should explicitly distinguish these historical witnesses from its parameter-matched controls; it currently leaves two plausible implementations of the main comparison.

P16 is parameterized, despite its fixed post geometry. `s4_escort_probe_v3/escort.h:8–10` constructs the P12/v6 base with the supplied parameters; `dispatch.cpp:23–25` passes the profile parameters through. The witnessed v3/v4 policies and request templates use historical v6 attempt-2 stage-B knobs. P16 retains v6 ranged firing targets, complete melee actions, complete post-battery fallthrough and the gun firing target when no enemy gun is reachable (`s4_escort_probe_v3/escort.cpp:14–17,27–29,42`; `s4_escort_probe_v1/escort.cpp:35–38`). Its internal target memory and dynamics are also v6's. These outputs can change with tuned knobs even with every outer gate forced to commit.

Therefore byte identity to historical P16 on historical-knob observations does not prove identity to the always-commit version of tuned v7. Comparing tuned v7 against historical-knob P16 would measure both switching and changes in the inherited controller. This is an unresolved specification, not evidence that such a mismatched run has occurred.

**Concrete fix:** define one selected vector θ* from v7 tuning, then name the validation arms `v7(θ*)`, `v7 forced-P16(θ*)`, `v7 forced-v6(θ*)`, and `v7 ω0(θ*)`. Force only the outer selector; continue normal z integration, internal target publication, pair-mode updates, pressure bookkeeping and failure handling. Use the same supplied θ* in both action-source computations. Historical P16(θ_v6) can remain a separately labelled descriptive witness if desired; it is not the matched control and needs an explicit additional fight budget. Distinguish preserved source identity from fixed parameter identity. Prove complete command/state agreement at multiple admissible knob vectors on observation sequences, including no reachable gun, no battery, degenerate escort direction and melee. Do not integrate one state twice per tick to obtain the two actions; use one captured baseline plus overlays, or independently identical controller states advanced once each.

## R2-F2 — High: the improvement reading is impossible near P16's witnessed performance

`DESIGN_0G.md:1512` requires at least four extra regular wins **and** improvement in at least 12 of 20 clusters. Each cluster contains only two binary orientation outcomes. A positive cluster difference requires P16 to have at least one nonwin in that cluster. Thus 12 positive clusters require at least 12 P16 nonwins: **P16 must win at most 28/40 for this reading ever to pass**, regardless of v7's quality.

At P16's replicated 34/40 performance, even v7 winning 40/40 cannot meet that condition. The actual replication table has P16 nonwins only in c10, c14, c15 and c17 (`s4_escort_probe_v4/S4_ESCORT_PROBE.md:33,37–40`); only four clusters there can improve. This is an arithmetic counterexample using existing outcomes, not a proposal to rerun or reclassify the replication. The 12-of-20 rule was usable for P16 versus weak P12; it cannot be copied to v7 versus strong P16.

**Concrete fix:** declare a ceiling-aware descriptive reading before any v7 fight. For example, use the already stated net gain of at least four wins for an **observed improvement**, report positive/negative/tied clusters and orientation discordance, and keep the paired cluster interval as uncertainty rather than a population verdict. If consistency across clusters is additionally required, choose and justify a rule that permits improvement over a high-win baseline, with its denominator and zero-opportunity case declared. Do not change the rule after seeing validation. A saturated comparator should limit detectable headroom, not make improved performance a logical failure.

## R2-F3 — Medium: the knob ledger omits oscillator-dependent commit firing and fallthrough

`DESIGN_0G.md:1494` says the knobs act only through z/the gate and escape actions. The artillery commit row at 1526 says “none in the action (z only)”; the ranged commit row at 1528 says “none in the escort action.” Those descriptions are false if “action” means the complete `UnitDecision` promised at 1478–1479.

P16's unreachable-gun branch retains the v6 firing target. The escort changes only movement fields, preserving the v6 target in every ordinary escort tick (`s4_escort_probe_v1/escort.cpp:36`). v6 target scores and retention consume complex-state alignment and κ-scaled engagement (`native/s4_v6_controller.cpp:70–77`), so firing can change with K, K_t, κ, β, μ and ω. Complete melee commands are v6 in both outer modes. Gun-phase exit and unavailable escort direction also retain v6 commands; these use neighbour and enemy-motion terms as well as targeting. Those mechanisms exist in the always-P16 arm, not only escape mode.

**Concrete fix:** separate fixed **movement geometry** from inherited **firing selection**, internal target-memory dynamics and v6 fallthrough. Give explicit role/phase/fallback rows: reachable-gun P16 target ordering is fixed; unreachable-gun firing is v6; ranged escort movement is fixed but firing is v6; degenerate/no-battery and all melee commands are v6. Trace the five movement knobs through their actual role/enemy-role uses, including f_c's artillery and melee branches (`native/s3_controller.cpp:42–57`). State that the matched contrast tests the outer switching policy conditional on an oscillator-containing P16 package, not adding oscillators to a fully static controller. Preserve the current explicit exclusions for source recursion, hierarchy, synchrony causality and oscillation necessity.

## R2-F4 — Medium: the ω0 arm changes the whole inherited policy, not just gate timing

Lines 1484 and 1489 specify ω_ranged = 0 and call the contrast the contribution of rotation. This is a legitimate total parameter intervention at θ*, but it is not an isolated gate-timing intervention. Changing ω changes z, which changes target alignment, neighbour similarity, target-memory groups, subsequent pressure status and the selected fallback movement. Coupling can also change melee states and commands although melee's own natural rate remains zero (`native/s4_v6_complex.cpp:26–58`; `native/s4_v6_controller.cpp:40–42,62–77`). Even forced-P16 ranged firing and post-battery actions can differ.

**Concrete fix:** call this the **observed total effect of setting the inherited ranged/artillery natural-rate parameter to zero**, conditional on θ* and the shared package. Report gate transitions alongside firing/geometry changes; do not attribute all of the performance difference to the timing of committed posts or escort decisions. The matched forced-P16 contrast answers the outer gate question after R2-F1. If isolated timing is an additional objective, it needs a separately specified intervention holding the other action consumers fixed; that is new design scope, not silently supplied by ω0. If selected ω is already zero, disclose exact arm identity rather than treating duplicate results as evidence of necessity or equivalence.

## R2-F5 — Medium: complete the descriptive readings and remaining executable protocol details

The revision fixes the main novice-eligibility and rank-order problems: tuning permits ≥8/16 novice wins, while observed readiness requires ≥21/40 and positive S on both heads. The smaller 20-cluster validation is acceptable for the explicitly descriptive question; it is insufficient to infer superiority, equivalence or a population win rate above 50%.

Three remaining seams need explicit handling:

1. **Unclassified outcomes:** +4 or more total wins with fewer than 12 positive clusters fits neither “adds,” “matches” nor “worse” at 1512–1514. R2-F2 should remove the incompatible condition; otherwise add an explicit descriptive/mixed case. “Matches” means only a count difference within the declared band, not statistical equivalence. Replace “the gate adds” with “observed improvement” to agree with the no-inferential-verdict boundary.
2. **Comparator floor versus absolute readiness:** “none of these readings” at 1516 also disables v7's own observed owner criterion when P16 has fewer than 21 wins. That floor should govern witness-relative categories only. Report every raw outcome and the independent v7 per-head criterion even if the comparator fails its floor; flag comparator underperformance separately. This does not authorize S5 or prove a population property.
3. **Exact optimizer bookkeeping:** define candidate index as a monotonically increasing evaluation ordinal, or explicitly use metric-only incumbent comparison with earlier-evaluation retention. Generation-local index plus “best over all generations” can replace an equally performing incumbent with a later generation's smaller index. The inherited v6 tuner evaluates its starting vector before the 256 generated candidates (`astelia_cpp/s4_v6.py:222–225`), while §20.1 budgets exactly 256×32 fights. Say whether that initial 32-fight evaluation is included, omitted or separately budgeted, and whether it can remain incumbent. Pin the executable optimizer contract: normalized knob ordering/bounds, σ=0.25 from `s4_v6.py:41–45`, optimizer version/seed, identical rank ordering into CMA and retention, and the fixed bootstrap seed/interval construction. Extend the stated fake-record checks to cross-generation ties, start-vector accounting, comparator ceiling/floor, strict novice/readiness boundaries and failure rows. No project test or fight is requested by this review.

## Nonblocking implementation notes

**N1 — Mixed modes and pair memory are deterministic, but “escape” names a source arm rather than a universal retreat.** No pair decides a P16 post or escort. However, v6's source command still uses continuous kite branches and per-pair hysteresis. An old pair can remain escaping inside the deadband while a newly encountered equal-range pair initializes committed at c=0 (`native/s3_controller.cpp:50–57`), even while the outer per-unit mode retains “escape.” Complete v6 may therefore advance or hold. Do not synchronize or overwrite pair modes to make the outer name literal: that breaks check 2 and the declared action source. Label telemetry by selected source and retain pair-mode diagnostics. Fixture both histories and target death/reassignment.

**N2 — All-living assignment is a disclosed interaction, not an implementation contradiction.** A committed ranged unit may follow the observed centre of an escaping gun; a committed gun without a reachable enemy gun may approach an anchor made reachable only by an escaping gun; committed guns repel from escaping guns too. Those actions still equal their declared source commands on the snapshot. Add explicit mixed-mode fixtures for these cases, including gun death, nearest-gun reassignment and battery-phase exit, and measure escort-to-assigned-gun mode combinations, post arrival and gun survival. Do not silently restrict candidate sets to committed units: that is another action map requiring revision. The policy need not enforce a coordinated battery to be executable.

**N3 — Preserve the exact witness's limitations.** P16 has P11 gun repulsion and unshifted P12 ranged escorts; there is no P15 ranged spacing in this revision. Clarify the sentence “P11 for guns and P12 for ranged” at 1518 so it does not imply ranged repulsion. P11 can preserve multiplier zero despite a nonzero spacing shift; spacing and native clipping can consume the 12 px nominal margin and move goals outside the firing annulus. These are inherited, explicitly allowed behaviours, not new violations. Cover full targets/goals/multipliers/stops/failure flags, raw and native-clipped gun goals, coincidence, boundaries, degenerate direction and no-gun fallthrough. Melee is unchanged v6; the two outer sources are identical for melee, so its outer mode has no direct action-selection effect, though melee continues to influence allies.
