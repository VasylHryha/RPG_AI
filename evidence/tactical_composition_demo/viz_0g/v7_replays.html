The pattern across these six

The fight is a race between the two gun lines, decided between about 17 and 34 s. In the wins all ten enemy guns are dead by 26–33 s. In the losses our guns die first (all gone by 28–34 s) while 2–5 enemy guns survive. When our guns are gone, the survivors switch to escape and run for the map edge. Then they either get hunted down or hide until the 150 s limit.
