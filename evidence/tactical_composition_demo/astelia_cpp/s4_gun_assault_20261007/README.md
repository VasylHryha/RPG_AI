Raw telemetry schema (version 1):

- Each observerV1 tick has step, t, units, actions, damage, launches, dodges and entries. Tick 0 is the initial state. The terminal host summary is the last line. `units` includes living active units only; damage records preserve lethal identities even after the body disappears from living state.
- Unit array: id, team, role index, x, y, hp, radius, range, minRange, hasSlot, slotX, slotY, prep, currentTargetId (null if unresolved). Role indices: melee 0, ranged 1, artillery 2, hunter 3, archer 4, player 5.
- Action array: id, team, target (0 if absent), goalX, goalY, multiplier, stop, release enum, move, keep, post, bound, inReach. Release enums retain native types.h values. Both armies' actions are observed, not recomputed.
- Damage is named fields: source/sourceRole/sourceTeam, target/targetRole/targetTeam, mitigated amount, capped HP dealt, time, exact centre distance, exact source/target positions, died and killer (source ID on a lethal event, null otherwise). Valid zero-amount applications are included; invalid/missing/dead targets return without applying damage. Amount may exceed dealt on overkill. Sources can be dead projectile owners; friendly damage is retained.
- For lethal enemy-gun damage, lethalGunInReach and lethalGunInsideBand contain exact living own unit IDs at the application boundary; lethalGunSupport rows are supporting enemy gun id, x, y, range, minRange at that same boundary. These augment the preceding-three-second tick-snapshot series. Legal reach is range+radii for melee/ranged, centre range with minRange for artillery. It does not mean an attack was released or that its lane was clear.
- Launch array: sourceId, currentTargetId (0 if missing), team, aimX, aimY, native born, arrivalTime, splash, barrage, slow. The containing tick's t is authoritative launch time; ability shells retain their historical born value. Current target is an intended-target observation, not proof of where an area shell will land or who receives its damage. Slow field shells are excluded from 'shelling another target'.
- Dodge array: unit id, team, fromX, fromY, chosenGoalX, chosenGoalY. These are successful returns of the original shell/field dodgeGoal routine. Multiple returns for the same unit/tick remain multiple calls, not distinct units.
- Entry named fields identify an own directed native move/ability crossing from outside an enemy gun's maximum range into its inclusive legal band, with displacement toward that gun. It stores exact source/gun geometry, other-gun positions, other-band cover count, and exact own within-reach/in-band IDs. Collision-only separation and a gun moving toward a stationary unit are outside the declared approach definition.

Sampling/denominators: all 120 fights and every emitted tick are used. Time bins are [0,20), [20,60), [60,end]; gun-only is a separately overlapping predicate with no enemy non-gun alive before the tick. Each seed cluster has the two orientations, and shares entropy across arms/heads for descriptive matching. All cells contain ten clusters and twenty fights. Observed geometry means weight ticks within a fight, then weight fights equally in the cell. Kill-distance means weight kills. Conditional win-time means exclude losses/timeouts, so null means no elimination wins. Approach outcomes are pair episodes; unique start IDs over the preceding three seconds measure temporally nearby starts, not exact simultaneity or phase-locking. Exact entry/kill snapshots and surviving posttick concurrency are retained separately.

No causal profile ablation was run. Regular line/dodge/lead/ability components remain bundled. Small-sample outcomes are not acceptance or qualification. docs/PLAN_CURRENT.md is owner-maintained and was not edited; local review tracking is PLAN_AND_REVIEW.md.
