## 1. Battlefield and unit observation contract

The roster source is [s4_shape_lab_v2/lab.py:86–99](../s4_shape_lab_v2/lab.py), reused by the [v4 requests](../s4_shape_lab_v4/requests.py) and [v6 requests](../s4_shape_lab_v6/requests.py). Both sides start with **50 units: 10 melee/brutes, 30 ranged/spitters and 10 artillery/shamans**. Regular and every C3 tactic use this same roster; tactics alter behavior rather than army size. The arena is 1400×800 px, Rules::Game, at 30 physical ticks/s, with a 150-game-second limit. No Player role or ability policy is admitted. Enemies retain native regular/tactic control, movement, fire and shell evasion; none are stationary drill scaffolds.

The [v6 declaration](../s4_shape_lab_v6/DECLARATION.json) names the 19 pool tactics: line, wide line, wedge, box, column, loose, screen, crescent, ring, wedge hold, line anvil, wedge flank, loose free, swarm, loose skirmish, loose berserk, storm, wolfpack and alone. C3 means regular plus those 19, twenty cells in total. Series draw uniformly with replacement from the 19 pool tactics, matching v6; regular is measured separately. Every request records the realized roster, orientation, map/placement seed, rules and opponent configuration.

Use a new strict versioned schema, AS1. Each unit receives an egocentric public view, with masks, through a value-only object rather than a World pointer. The initial entity support is **self + 64 nearest other own entities + 64 nearest enemies**. Distance then stable identity routes ties, with identity retained as join metadata and not a numeric learned feature. For the standard roster all 49 friends and all 50 enemies fit, so k-nearest ordering does not remove a distant gun, escort partner or splash cluster. Pointer heads have **none + 64** slots, never none + 50. A missing/dead start-time or cached target is a separate historical reference, not a living enemy token and not target-none. It is not locked through preparation. Counts, centroids and masks retain absent-role information as survivors dwindle.

Entity tokens contain role, relative position, velocity, HP/maxHP, body radius, speed, weapon range/minimum range, damage, cooldown/cycle, current publicly visible target and targeting-self mask. Own tokens additionally contain preparation, windup, local time rate, energy/cost, guard/busy state, projectile speed, splash, attack readiness, last start/launch/consume and current action-phase flags. Self contains the complete pending action cache: absolute goal/aim expressed relative to current self, start-time versus current target references, cache age, expiry, readiness origin and ready-hold age. Enemy energy, hidden intents, private smoothing, skills, plan scores and RNG are absent. Public enemy committed casts use the same advertised geometry/timing convention as [react.cpp:25–40](../s4_react_adapter_v1/react.cpp); they are estimates, not future trajectories.

Threats have separate masked banks: **128 hostile tokens** for shells, aimed shots, fields and advertised casts, and **64 own-shell tokens** for joint splash accounting. Own shells cannot evict enemy threats. Tokens preserve kind, point/direction, impact or release time, radius, speed/distance, damage, source/target reference and slow flag; own/enemy masks are explicit. Hostile relevance orders intersection with self/current reachable movement support first, then time to contact, distance and source order. Own shells order by impact time and distance. These are initial capacity choices, not measured maxima. Count omissions by kind and decision, and compare the retained-view oracle against the full-public oracle on the same state. Any omitted threat that changes an active label blocks data admission until support is expanded prospectively. Leader support must also retain every shell used by its planner label; an overflowing leader snapshot cannot be silently trained as though complete.

No tactic name, doctrine ID, teacher family, score, fight/seed ID or privileged channel enters prediction. Enemy cumulative private bookkeeping is not imported to imitate v6 memory. Wire key allowlists, finite-value checks, masks, reflection/inverse reflection, pointer rebinding and precision are part of the future contract. Reordering wire entities must reorder physical bindings without changing non-tied commands. Exact indistinguishable identity ties are counted and their identity-specific labels masked explicitly, never presented as learned coordination.

## 2. Unit policies and labels that units can decide

Choose **a shared 64-wide entity encoder with a learned role embedding and separate melee/ranged/artillery output heads**, rather than three unrelated networks. Two masked attention blocks pool own, enemy and threat tokens; target scores use token pointers. Capacity and actual parameter count will be sealed before fitting. The plain unit policy U-P has bounded eight-coordinate recurrent memory. U-R replaces that memory with the NS1-derived bounded phase state and local geometry coupling; it does not append a second plain recurrent state. Both use identical physical inputs, neighbor access, command support, loss masks and data order. The local neighbor graph is nearest eight within 300 px, while attention still sees the complete represented entity sets.

U-R uses the phase law in [dynamics.py:11–39](../s4_net_slice_v1/dynamics.py), with physical positions supplied by the engine, degree-normalized exp(−(distance/100 px)^2) coupling, bounded forcing, |omega|≤2 rad/s, K≤2 rad/s, RK4 and the same substep/stage checks in training and C++. Phase influences learned target/move/fire heads, and realized movement changes subsequent geometry. **J=0, with no additional analytic drift in either learned arm**: the old untrained J=.5 cohesion is not carried forward. Learned movement can close the local loop; no loop use or coupling advantage is claimed from imitation. Initial phases come from public physical geometry and role by a fixed bounded recipe, not ID modulo 16. Actual attack/launch acknowledgements reset phase by the same declared rule in replay and deployment, and reset is separately identified as an action-to-mode channel.

The full script runs the teacher fights. On each recorded public state a separate **autonomous role oracle** supplies unit labels, with commands absent. It reconstructs only the encoded view at stored precision. It cannot take the full script's hidden v6 state, a joint artillery queue or another gun's assigned aim as a unit label. P16 inherits history-dependent v6 behavior ([s4_v6_controller.cpp:25–86](../src/native/s4_v6_controller.cpp)); copying its raw actions as snapshot-decidable labels would repeat the earlier error. The autonomous oracle is therefore an explicitly declared public decomposition of the best script, rather than a claim of exact imitation of every internal P16 action. Record the original script action, autonomous label and their disagreement, separately by role/head and cause. The script's actual full-fight performance remains the comparator.

| Role/head | Decidable autonomous label and action |
|---|---|
| All: target | Choose a legal living enemy from the represented set at a base decision, including during preparation, matching native retarget semantics. Melee/ranged rank nearest legal enemy, then distance to legal reach if none is reachable. Artillery ranks reachable enemy guns by splash value, then low HP, then distance; if no enemy gun is reachable, choose the nearest supported engagement target. All scoring quantities are public represented geometry. Identity only routes exact ties. |
| All: movement | Public represented react takes precedence; otherwise melee closes to body-gap weapon reach, ranged keeps its public engagement band, and artillery keeps the legal min/max annulus. The band uses current weapon ranges/body radii and a fixed 1 px interior margin, not a hidden role-specific fit. Units approach when outside reach and do not abandon combat for low HP. Quantize a bounded one-second goal to hold + 16 directions at .6/full speed. Report requested-to-supported movement error. |
| Melee: attack | Predict separate start and release permissions on eligible own-body/resource/reach rows. No artillery aim head or volley slot is active. Actual native melee impact remains physics authority. |
| Ranged: attack | Predict separate start and release permissions, plus target. Native ordinary projectile mechanics determine the shot. No fabricated gun-aim supervision; dodge is learned movement. |
| Artillery: start/release | Decide from this gun's cooldown, energy, body, represented threats, target reach and preparation. Readiness of another gun never supplies an autonomous wait label. Preserve the current act's target/aim and distinguish permission, consumed cast and actual shell launch. |
| Artillery: aim | Choose this gun's own best feasible single shot on its selected target, without a joint spread. Compute public raw-velocity lead by three distance/lob fixed-point iterations, then bound to arena and gun annulus. From a finite 33-point support choose the best single-shell public hit/damage estimate; include the exact feasible lead centre and local splash rings. This is a declared public single-shot model, not an oracle future hit or a globally optimal shot. |

The public splash score must be specified once in AS1 using enemy/friendly overlap, damage and body radii, not read from hidden planner scores. Solo aim candidates may include cluster offsets inferred from this gun's complete enemy tokens; they never read peer assignments. Candidate construction gives feasible choices; the **network chooses** target, movement, attack timing and aim. It does not replace an incorrect prediction with the script's chosen shot. Mask nonfinite/unsupported opportunities with counted reasons. Report analytic lead residual, raw-to-feasible bound distance, supported error and actual landing; residual above splash/4 on any finite unforced release row calls for an oracle revision before fitting. Intentional group spread is evaluated as assignment fidelity and splash coverage, not as centre-seeking miss.

Autonomous and command-conditioned labels are different views of the same state. The conditioned oracle uses only that unit's encoded observation plus the received command and observable lifecycle/cache fields. Give move and artillery-aim heads **34 fixed slots**: the 33 autonomous candidates plus one optional, exactly reconstructed command candidate. The extra slot contains the feasible bounded waypoint or exact feasible assigned aim point, and is masked when the command is absent/illegal. A commanded focus is already represented in the none+64 target head. This keeps a V2 point from being lost in a polar grid. The unit network still selects that slot; the host never overwrites its output with the command. Unit obedience labels select the exact command slot only on feasible present-command opportunities, while the autonomous branch uses the original 33-way labels. Log original teacher waypoint, bounded waypoint and bound distance separately. Equal aggregate loss mass goes to empty and present views, with deterministic legal command augmentation on training fights only. A release-event command cannot retarget the already selected current act. The group oracle's assignments never become empty-command labels. No group timing is imitated in stage B; timing outputs remain structurally masked.

Before training, hash the **exact stored encoded input including masks, pending context and command**, excluding identity/seed metadata, and count unequal active labels among identical inputs. Report conflicts, collision groups, compared rows and inactive/undefined counts per role/head/split and command presence. The requirement is **zero exact active-label conflicts**, checked on collected data. Also report one .02-normalized-unit bin diagnostic with occupancy. Zero conflicts with few or no repeated inputs is not a proof; dependency inspection and replay of the oracle from serialized inputs are required. Duplicate cross-split trajectories are a separate data gate. An oracle that consults unencoded information is invalid even if no sample collides.

## 3. Neutral host and action schedule

The learned controller derives directly from the neutral Controller bridge. The [react adapter dispatch:5–8](../s4_react_adapter_v1/dispatch.cpp) instantiates S4V7Controller before commands, so it is **teacher-only**; submitting a network through it would leave scripted P16/react policy active. Learned arms have no inherited P16/v7/react/E1/R1/planner, native own formation brain or hidden nearest-target rescue. Enemy control remains unchanged. Native collision, damage and range/body/resource gates remain shared, with any own-policy reflex explicitly disabled or identically declared for all arms. The full-army bridge rewrites target on dispatch ([controller_bridge.cpp:39–53](../src/native/controller_bridge.cpp)); preparation consumes resources/progress without saving a target ([combat_rules.cpp:60–68](../src/native/combat_rules.cpp)). AS1 therefore does **not** copy NS1's custom start-target lock or six-tick forced release/cancel. Log target at start, current dispatch target and aim reference separately.

Base unit decisions occur every six physical ticks. State advances once per physical tick. Goals and aim requests are absolute cached points, not offsets repeatedly applied to moving bodies. **After native preparation progress and before act**, obtain a fresh snapshot and release/aim readout for every physically eligible ready unit and the joint ready battery, even between base decisions. Repeat on **every eligible held-ready physical tick** until release or loss of opportunity; first-ready-only sampling would strand a learned hold. The event readout does not integrate unit/leader state twice. Its frame and held-input age are logged and included in training. Both leaders see the same event support. It avoids the four-tick late-refresh defect identified in S1_WRAPPER_DIAGNOSIS while matching v6's post-preparation/pre-act placement. Count every event row when projecting data and runtime; the 5 Hz row bound alone is insufficient.

The ordering is pre-preparation snapshot → once-only state update → base autonomous proposal/direct publication → optional base leader command → conditioned base heads → dispatch movement/current target/start permission → native preparation progress → fresh ready-event snapshot/proposal → optional battery leader command → conditioned release/aim heads → native act → impact/launch/consume acknowledgement. **State updates use current physical observation and previously accepted cache context, but exclude the new command being produced on this tick.** Autonomous and conditional heads use that same updated state; neither unit recurrence nor phase advances a second time. Leader state advances once too. Event observation refreshes head inputs and next-step forcing without a second integration. Replay stores both pre-preparation and post-preparation frames, held age, previous cache, current commands, heads and acknowledgements. At release events, target and movement are held to the current base dispatch for that single act; V2's aim-reference enemy is **not a retarget command**. A later base decision may retarget a still-preparing unit under source semantics. Absolute group points may differ from the current target but satisfy v6's conservative current/post-movement gun annulus and arena predicate. The actual role mechanics use pre-walk target reach for melee/artillery and post-walk reach for ranged ([combat.cpp:11–33](../src/native/combat.cpp)); do not replace them with a universal post-walk gate. Future fixtures must exercise this deployed order, including a gun becoming prepared between base decisions.

Learned attack start/release requires its corresponding observed opportunity, rather than allowing a cached positive permission to authorize a newly arising opportunity. Start permission gates the first preparation/resource-consumption request, without cancelling progress already paid for. Release uses the current event aim; target/aim are fixed only through that act's launch/consume, then become historical acknowledgements. **No extra forced release, ready timeout or resource refund is introduced.** Held-ready duration and attack throughput diagnose hold collapse; the neutral host does not rescue it with a script. New threats are neural inputs, while actual body/resource/invalid-point vetoes remain mechanics. Teacher movement/targets run at their source physical cadence, learned base movement/targets at six-tick cadence with ready events; disclose that teacher cadence advantage and measure action-support/cadence projection differences on recorded states. This is a practical teacher-gap comparison, not a claim of controller equivalence from a document.

Malformed/nonfinite output fails the affected invocation visibly. Expired, unsupported or conflicting **validly parsed** commands become empty with a reason, and units then use their autonomous network branch. Body/resource/range rejection, missing current targets and consumed-without-launch are counted separately from policy hold. Empty-command replay must reproduce U-alone state, goals, targets, permissions and aim to atol=rtol=1e−9. Shadow labelling cannot change live state, caches, RNG or outcomes.

This is the smallest way to preserve global P16 escort relationships and V2's ten-gun joint assignment. No dynamically detected groups, formation acceptance, multi-parent hierarchy or separate commander per role is required. The RRG parent is specifically over the artillery children; own infantry are sibling context, not hidden descendants of those guns.

Record full teacher planner provenance: ready-gun set, chosen family/variant, generated centre/anchor reference, exact assigned point, gun binding, assignment-time frame, legal/rejected status, Singles and leftovers. Family and scores are training/audit targets or metadata, never input features. Canonical public insertion order preserves source binding and route ties; no Hungarian machinery is needed. Planner fidelity checks compare generated points before projection, supported points, accepted native points and actual launches on the same state. A shape's deliberate offset must not fail an autonomous-intercept miss gate, the R3 Claude recheck's M1 lesson.

Commands carry optional masks for **escort/approach waypoint**, **base-decision focus**, and **release-stage aim point with explicit aim reference**. Presence is predicted per unit/type; waypoint is bounded to the arena and one-second movement support, aim to arena/annulus, focus to none+64 enemy pointers. Cache age, expiry, current-act phase and the resolved point are visible to the receiving unit. Gun/plan IDs route outstanding messages without becoming numeric network inputs. In stage B Singles/leftover guns get **empty aim commands** and keep their own best single shot. No group shift is trained: per-unit react with a larger threat set is not a joint group reaction. No fire-together/hold-start head is trained from ready-only V2 labels.

P is a plain leader with two 64-wide masked set-attention blocks, invariant pooled context, bounded eight-coordinate recurrent group memory, and shared unit command heads. R uses the identical encoder, inputs, heads and output bounds, but **replaces** plain group memory with persistent parent phase Phi and refreshed direct-child geometry/mode/port composition. It does not nest P's recurrence inside R. Match learned parameter counts within 10%, choosing fixed widths before fitting; report steps, rows, parameters, FLOPs estimate, measured wall/CPU and peak/aggregate RSS separately. Equal parameters do not imply equal cost.

Each unit publishes its actual position/velocity, size, HP, readiness, opportunities, provisional and current-act action, command/cache validity, and a bounded direct mode. U-R publishes sin/cos(theta) and its bounded natural rate. U-P exposes the corresponding bounded coordinates of its own recurrent state, with a mask stating their nonoscillatory meaning; these are real state publications, not auxiliary fitted heads. Both P and R receive **the same publications for a fixed child checkpoint**. Joint leader imitation uses pooled chronological public histories from each frozen child checkpoint, relabelled by the same stateless group teacher, with equal total weight per child family. Thus the leaders receive the child context they will use and no leader receives the teacher's hidden v6 state.

For artillery children use area weights w_i=r_i²/sum(r_j²), X=sum(w_i p_i), L²=sum(w_i(|p_i−X|²+r_i²)), and m=sum(w_i exp(i theta_i)). Parent natural rate is the same area-weighted child rate; port capacities are sums of direct-child eligible attack rates. Phi initializes to arg(m) when |m|≥1e−6, otherwise a fixed zero with a coherence mask, and persists while live membership changes. The proposed parent law is Phi_dot = omega_parent + f_parent(context) + K_parent×sum_i[w_i×exp(−|p_i−X|²/(100 px)²)×sin(theta_i−Phi)]. Bound the learned forcing to ±1 rad/s and K_parent to [0,2] rad/s, using the unit law's rate transforms and RK4/substep/stage envelope; |Phi_dot|≤5 rad/s. Hold published geometry and child modes during each integration step. The encoder/readout differs by level, but these physical bounds and normalization do not get level-specific tuning. Its sin/cos drives command heads; resulting child movement/aim changes physical geometry and subsequent publications. U-P's published mode angle is atan2 of its first two bounded memory coordinates, absent below norm 1e−6; its natural-rate publication is absent rather than an invented oscillation rate. Contexts with U-P children train shared command interfaces, while RRG parent-dynamics interpretation applies only to actual U-R children. A zero/empty group emits empty commands and invalid composition, with no fabricated parent. Direct invalid children can veto composition; deaths prune identity-bound state, while every living child continues evolving.

This implements a proposed level-2 resonator interface, not proof of an effective higher-level unit. CURRENT reports the audited v0.2.1 source as VERIFIED; implementation must verify the relevant immutable source bytes before source-qualified execution. Inherited capacities and natural rates require full-versus-coarse transfer evidence before a next-level dynamics claim. Causal background transformation B_n → R_n → B_(n+1), H-U/H-COMP/H-BG/H-PS, spontaneous group formation and source recursion remain NOT_TESTED.

For pooled training rows with U-P children, define the numerical interface completely: absent rates store zero **with rate-present=false**, absent angles store zero with mode-present=false and are excluded from coherence/coupling sums, and parent-dynamics-valid=false. Zero is a computational placeholder, not an inherited rate. R does not integrate its parent phase on these contexts; Phi is neutral zero with a false phase-valid mask, while its shared command heads still train from physical/public context and any present child-mode tokens. Each family context has a separate reset/history, so a U-P row cannot erase a live U-R parent state. For U-R contexts require valid direct modes/rates before using the stated parent law. Thus pooled command training remains defined without turning plain recurrent coordinates into claimed child resonators.

## 6. Data and training, using NS1 tooling patterns

Stage A teacher fights use the full measured script, regular and all twenty C3 cells, with fresh development entropy. Start with **240 whole fights**, allocated **60 regular + 180 pool fights**, balancing pool cells to 9 or 10 as a sealed inventory. Allocate by opponent and orientation before execution; split whole fights into 180 training, 30 validation and 30 test, ensuring each of twenty cells is represented in every split. Regular is intentionally oversampled and weights are declared. Teacher/group/raw action and autonomous/conditioned labels are recorded together; stage B reuses these fights instead of commissioning another script-value panel. If a required role/head/command lacks coverage, record it as unresolved and revise the next data batch prospectively.

A **≤20 physical-fight timing sample** is selected from this sealed training inventory before execution, one per opponent cell, with orientations balanced. It measures full teacher collection, record/frame/row sizes, event frequency, peak RSS, output disk and elapsed/CPU time. Completed sample fights stay in their original training split and are reused, not repeated. No fight runs in this design session. A new scope needs its own approval before this sample. Before all remaining collection, project the entire 240-fight extent using per-stratum measured seconds/frame/row, maximum-duration equivalents and a disclosed 20% allowance; show the uncertainty from one sample/cell. Physical partial/failed attempts count toward the authorized extent and remain preserved. Do not infer a full-army cost from the old seven-minute gun drill.

Use independent entropy pools for training inventory, mechanism/sanity, regular outcomes, C3 outcomes and series. ## 7. Paired readouts and useful decisions

The required arms are **T** (full source teacher), **U-P alone**, **U-R alone**, **U-P+P**, and **U-R+R**. Add **U-R+P** as the one necessary bridge arm. Comparing U-P+P with U-R+R changes both levels and cannot identify a leader effect. U-R+R versus U-R+P holds the child checkpoint fixed; each versus U-R alone measures leader addition. U-P+P versus U-P alone checks the plain system. No-leader arms supply exactly empty commands, not random/malformed messages. Teacher is labelled script/search, the other five are labelled trained network with their mechanism variant stated.

One early **10–20 matched full-army battle set per arm** checks mechanism/competence before large outcomes. Begin with ten pairs: five regular and five predeclared artillery-heavy/evasive pool cases, balanced orientation; extend to twenty only for a specific coverage uncertainty. Stage A runs **T, the public autonomous role oracle O, U-P and U-R** on its own independent sanity inventory (40–80 physical fights). A bad decomposition is revised rather than answered with more network fitting. This does not require another broad script-value panel or add O to outcome arms. Stage B's six-arm set includes the frozen no-leader controls and new independent draws (60–120 physical fights). These are sanity observations, not dropping candidates because of a four-fight win count. Report per-role start and release per opportunity, actual attacks/launches, target/no-target, in-range participation, dodges/threat response, deaths by killer role, friendly damage, shell coverage and unsupported/projection rates. Where a required role has <20 eligible attack or reaction opportunities, competence is unresolved. A start/release rate <half the same-state autonomous oracle's rate is a diagnosed learning gap. Do not divide launches by arbitrary tick counts.

For the leader report per-type eligible command counts, accepted commands, command-conditioned learned response, legal native realization and changed point/waypoint versus the **same-state autonomous shadow**, not cross-arm divergent tick matching. Each required type needs ≥20 eligible commands and ≥95% correct feasible response before claiming the interface works: focus succeeds when the conditional pointer is dispatched at the base decision, waypoint/aim succeeds when the exact feasible command slot is selected and its point reaches the native action within parity tolerance. Body/resource/death vetoes remain counted exclusions. At least 5% of eligible battery multi-gun events must contain a nonempty multi-gun aim assignment; otherwise coordination is NOT_EXERCISED. C3 gets a separate 100–200-pair panel total across twenty tactics**, not 200 per tactic. Wins are descriptive because the teacher is near saturation. Broken/incomplete pairs and partial costs remain visible; never silently select only wins or replace failed draws with favorable new ones.

## 10. Closest methods and the RRG difference

[AlphaStar's author description](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphastar-mastering-the-real-time-strategy-game-starcraft-ii/) uses a transformer torso over entities and pointer policy heads. That motivates entity encoders, masks and target pointers here; this slice does not reproduce AlphaStar's game scope, league or RL training. [FeUdal Networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/vezhnevets17a.html) separate manager goals and worker actions, providing a precedent for optional commander/unit control. [CommNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.07736) learns continuous communication among cooperative agents; [BiCNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.10069) uses bidirectional coordination and shared multi-agent actor-critic control in StarCraft combat. Those are communication/control precedents, not evidence for the present oscillator. [Expert Iteration](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08439) alternates search improvement and apprentice learning, motivating the stage-2 boundary.

The proposed RRG distinction is structural: a parent resonator has persistent mode, composed direct-child geometry/modes/ports and lawful downward commands that affect subsequent child geometry. A generic attention/communication policy alone does not establish that mechanism. The practical plain/RRG comparison holds the units fixed; an RRG-state claim additionally requires an exercised temporal task and causal channel controls. This slice claims neither RRG superiority nor effective-unit formation, source recursion, teacher optimality, future streak gains, release/deployment acceptance or performance qualification. It delivers a reviewable route to a complete trained full-army fight.
