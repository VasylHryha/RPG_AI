## 2. Unit contract: competent with an empty command

### Observation and causal schedule

Retain the NS1 public allowlist, masks, egocentric orientation, 12 friend/12 enemy slots, 8 threat slots and 1008 features. IDs remain join keys, never numeric network features. No leader slot, planner family, hidden opponent state or future trajectory enters the base observation. Pending native target/aim locks, six-physical-tick cadence, opportunity-only permissions, automatic prepared-release bound, actual-launch acknowledgements and native legality remain as in the contract. Scaffolds and abilities remain unchanged.

**Necessary correction:** the unit label oracle operates on the same encoded information as the unit, reconstructed from the retained slots and masks. In particular its REACT labels see only the retained eight threats. Full-public teacher reactions and threat overflow remain evaluator diagnostics, never undisclosed unit-label inputs. Record relevant threat omissions; this narrower teacher is explicitly a representation-constrained unit oracle. If loss of an omitted field prevents a required action, revise the allowlist in an approved schema amendment before recollection, rather than adding hidden state to labels.

On each decision tick the schedule is acyclic: (1) advance each unit's state once using tick-start geometry and previous accepted assignments; (2) compute the unit's provisional target with empty command, respecting native pending locks; (3) publish that proposed target/readiness at the direct-unit boundary; (4) leader consumes those publications and emits commands; (5) unit move/start/release/aim readouts consume the command, with target fixed to its own provisional choice; (6) commit intents simultaneously, then native mechanics act. The second readout does not advance recurrence/phase a second time. Units-alone perform the same schedule with command absent. Physical-tick state updates between decisions use held features/commands; there is no faster leader firing channel.

### Heads and single-shot labels

The empty-command oracle is a deterministic function `U(o_i)`:

| Head | Empty-command label and information used |
|---|---|
| target (none + 12 pointers) | nearest supported living enemy by distance, geometric tie order; native pending target retained/masked when locked |
| move (33) | own observable threat reaction, otherwise engagement-annulus goal on chosen target, participation projection, nearest supported move candidate |
| start (binary) | own chosen-target reach, body/resource/cooldown/prep opportunity and own observable reaction; no dependence on how many friends are ready |
| release (binary) | own projected readiness at this tick and body/reaction/opportunity, with native aim lock respected |
| aim (33) | this gun's best supported single shot on its chosen target, as defined below; no volley slot, teammate reservation or joint shell score |

For stage 1b, **best single shot means closest legal supported aim to the engine raw-velocity lead on the chosen target**, not a claim of globally optimal hit probability. Use the engine artillery planner's lead horizon specialized to one gun: `tau_i = .8 * range_i / lob_i` seconds and `q_i = target_position + target_public_velocity * tau_i`. `src/native/artillery.cpp` uses this horizon and `led()` for planner candidates; its Game `Singles` candidate itself is target-centred, so we deliberately choose the raw-lead rule, not claim that Singles already supplies lead. Do not use private smoothed velocity, dodge RNG or joint `predictVolley` scoring. Quantize q_i to the legal aim candidate minimizing squared distance, ties lowest candidate index. Native locks override a later provisional aim; no legal candidate or target-none means undefined aim with a mask and reason. Record raw q_i, selected point, snap error and opportunity.

The old radius `min(200,splash)` can conceal lead when targets move. Preserve 33 candidates and their target-centred layout, but use radius `R_i = min(200 px, max(splash_i, |v_target| * tau_i))`, half/full rings plus centre. Radius is computed from public fields by the same recipe for all arms, never calibrated by level. Legality masks remain native arena and gun reach. Record lead outside support separately; if the 200 px support cap is inadequate, stop for a schema/support revision. The decoder creates candidates only: no scripted lead point replaces the network's chosen aim. Report against centre and nearest-lead baselines; a trivial head remains diagnostic.

A unit's own observation includes other public units but does not require a group planner. Every empty-command label uses only o_i: reconstruct the oracle from the serialized tensor, then compare with the collector's label. The collector cannot read the joint queue for a unit label. Equal encoded observations, target context and legal masks must produce equal labels (up to the declared tie rule); repeat oracle queries and permute wire ordering with the same canonicalization. Persistent IDs may resolve indistinguishable pointer ties as join metadata; they cannot select different physical aim/move labels for otherwise identical geometry.

**Collected-data decidability gate:** commit exact-tensor collision counts and label conflict rate for each active head; exact conflicts must be zero. Also report conditional entropy `H(label | binned o_i, legal mask, chosen target)` in bits, minority disagreement `1 - sum_b max_y n_by / sum_b n_b`, collision-bin occupancy and split/fight counts. Use fixed bins in normalized units (.01, .02, .05), exact boolean/category bins, separate opportunity/lock strata, and at least two distinct fights per collision bin. Fine bins with no collisions are reported as insufficient coverage, never proof. Coarse-bin entropy can reflect quantization boundaries; inspect conflicts against oracle determinism and nearest decision-boundary distance, without tuning bins to outcomes. Repeat with commands for conditional labels. Zero sample entropy alone is not a mathematical proof; the restricted oracle establishes dependency, the measures detect representation errors. Ambiguity caused by a missing variable returns to the drafter before fitting.

### Optional command and obedience

Command schema `NS1B-command`: presence mask; per-gun aim-offset vector relative to its chosen target (px); advisory volley-slot token; `free/hold/fire_together` token; group-shift vector (px over the existing 1 s goal horizon); decision-origin/expiry, gun and target identity, volley identity as join metadata. Validity and target-reference masks accompany numeric fields. Empty = presence zero, payload zero, free timing, zero shift, no slot. Malformed commands fail the schema gate; expired, dead-target or target-mismatched commands become empty and log a reason. No teacher/planner is called during network inference.

A command describes a requested action; it does not write a native intent directly. With a valid command, the **conditional oracle** uses its aim request as the desired point, a hold token as a permission veto, a fire-together token as a request subject to the unit's own readiness/reaction, and the shift as a requested movement contribution subject to own participation/safety and native locks. Its output is quantized to the same five heads. A present aim request expands the conditional candidate radius to `min(200 px, max(R_i, |command_aim_offset|))`, retaining centre and half/full16-direction rings; the same public/command recipe is used by oracle, decoder and all arms. The empty-command radius remains R_i. Native reach/arena masks and raw-to-quantized snap diagnostics still apply. It never starts an unready cast, cancels windup, changes a locked target/aim, suppresses an unavoidable automatic release or bypasses legality. Fire-together means common decision opportunity, not guaranteed simultaneous launches. The network learns this mapping from `(o_i, provisional target, command)`; conditional aim is never mislabeled as autonomous single-shot aim.

The single collection records empty-command labels **and** conditional labels for the same snapshots using teacher leader commands. Add deterministic command dropout: equal aggregate loss mass on empty and present command examples, drawn only from training rows; duplicated views are not extra physical fights or independent samples. Target loss applies once to the empty-command provisional pass. Movement/fire/aim conditional losses are separate from autonomous losses, with counts and loss weights sealed before training. DAgger later supplies student-leader command contexts, querying the conditional unit oracle on the actual visitor's provisional target. A target change rebuilds its aim candidates; teacher-target-conditioned offline scores and student-target closed-loop errors are explicitly distinct.

## 3. Leader contract and labels at the group level

### Group observation and permutation-consistent supervision

Leader observes a masked **set of direct gun publications** (the full permitted own-unit field table, including positions, radii, velocities, HP, readiness/prep/windup/time_rate, cooldown, body/resource state, current and provisional target references, lob/splash, validity and mode/message summaries), **public own non-gun scaffold context tokens**, the same public enemy set, and public in-flight own/enemy shells and cast/shot/field threats. Maximum ten guns, twelve own units including scaffolds, twelve enemies and 64 raw threats; overflow is a declared invalid/out-of-scope state until an approved representation handles it. Scaffold tokens are environmental context, not resonator children or controllable outputs. Their presence is necessary because the copied planner's own-damage score and participation can depend on their positions/HP. No tactic ID, teacher score or branch outcome is a feature. Inputs are gun-centred/group-centred relative quantities; object identities route pointers, not learned numeric values. Both leaders have identical access and output support.

Labels come from the sanitized **group teacher**, `project + copiedPlanner`, with the same public options (raw lead, robust .5, no physical rollout, eligible-ready guns only, no delayed queue). It plans from the supplied provisional target publications, not a hidden teacher-only target choice. Teacher collection uses autonomous-oracle proposals; DAgger uses visitor proposals. Record the unsnapped joint plan and its assignment before conditional unit quantization. Reconstruct the oracle solely from the serialized group representation, including scaffold context, at the same precision used for training storage. Apply exact-observation conflict checks and fixed-bin entropy/occupancy diagnostics to leader labels as well, comparing exchangeable sets under the declared matching. Identical permitted group input cannot consult extra native state or yield a conflicting physical command.

Choose a **canonical slot order** for this draft: guns sorted lexicographically by canonical public x/y followed by the complete sanitized public gun token and referenced target geometry (velocity, HP, radius, range/min-range, speed, damage, cooldown, prep/windup, time_rate, resource/body state, lob/splash and all remaining planner-relevant masks/fields); enemy/scaffold and threat tokens likewise use complete public tokens with geometric/time keys first. IDs are excluded from the physical sort key. Run the scratch planner with that canonical insertion order. Assign its queue point to the gun already bound by the planner, then scatter output to original gun identities. Do not independently rematch physical guns after the planner assignment: unequal gun ranges/readiness make arbitrary aim-set matching wrong. Slot is queue rank within the chosen target/volley, metadata with a presence mask; a slot is not an ID-derived oscillator phase. If exact indistinguishable gun ties remain across **all** planner-relevant public fields, supervise their commands as an exchangeable set with legal-edge Hungarian matching (cost in normalized aim/shift distance plus timing-token mismatch), rather than demand conflicting identity-specific slots. Mask tied slot-rank loss. Distinct guns retain planner assignment. Reordering wire tokens must permute output rows with no physical command change; symmetric ties are evaluated modulo the declared matching.

Per gun output heads: aim vector, optional slot category (up to ten), timing categorical free/hold/fire_together, and group-shift vector. Aim and slot are active only for a planner-assigned eligible gun. Unassigned/nonready guns receive free timing and empty aim/slot; no invented hold labels merely because they cannot shoot. Current planner queue is immediate: assigned guns receive fire_together at that decision tick. A genuine planner hold label is available only if a prospectively specified public teacher arbitration vetoes an otherwise ready opportunity; the current copied planner cannot teach delayed synchronization. Count such labels; absent hold examples mean that command is NOT_TRAINED and masked in deployment, not assumed learned.

For group reactions, the planner wrapper records the full-public per-gun REACT goals as **per-gun shift commands relative to the autonomous unit oracle goal, including its represented-threat reaction**. Define `shift = full_public_group_goal - autonomous_goal`, cap its norm to one second of gun travel, and apply legality/participation to `autonomous_goal + shift` before quantizing the conditional move label. Equal reactions yield zero shift; this prevents doubling a dodge already present at the unit level. Record both goals, bounded residual, projected goal and selected candidate. Decoder/runtime do not add shift after the conditional head has already represented it. The conditional oracle uses this same convention on any supplied command. These are group-level set outputs available to the leader; a shared rigid translation or a new group search is not invented by averaging opposing dodges. Planner spread, fire-together and reaction shifts have separate coverage. Stronger hold scheduling/shared-shift teacher logic needs its own approved amendment, not outcome-derived retrospective labels.

Leader vector outputs use an explicit radial map: for finite raw vector v, aim offset is `200 px * v/(1+|v|)` and shift is `gun_speed * 1 s * v/(1+|v|)`. Reject NaN/Inf before mapping; zero-speed shift is zero. Aim is quantized through the command-conditioned unit support and native legal masks, with unsupported/absent targets producing logged empty aim, not a substitute scripted shot. Record raw, bounded, quantized and native-projected values/errors separately. A teacher request outside the 200 px envelope or without an admissible supported aim blocks its label/collection for a support amendment; it cannot silently become a centre label. Boundary targets can approach but not exactly reach the radial map's outer bound; boundary-snap error is disclosed.

Leader losses: masked squared error for normalized aim/shift; categorical CE for slot/timing; equal head weights before active-count normalization, with tied-slot handling above. Unit/group loss totals remain separate; leader predictions never train against unit aim labels. Seal capacity, exact tensor layout, masked head counts and joint budget in the implementation contract before data execution; this draft does not pretend old 81-output exports support a leader.

### Plain leader and RRG level-2 leader

Plain leader P: two 64-wide masked set-attention blocks (four heads), invariant group pooling, and a shared per-gun readout using each token and pooled state. No learned positional embedding of array order. Add bounded eight-coordinate recurrent group memory so it is a strong temporal comparator; zero it in a separately named control. Match P/R learned parameter counts within 10%, and report actual counts, decision rows, steps, physical-tick work and wall/CPU/RSS. Matching information and steps does not assert equal FLOPs.

RRG leader R is **level 2: one parent group resonator over its living direct gun units**. It is not a set of gun-index oscillators renamed a leader. Upward publications form group position `X=sum w_i p_i`, size `L=sqrt(sum w_i (|p_i-X|^2 + radius_i^2))`, complex direct-mode mean `m=sum w_i exp(i theta_i)`, mode dispersion, readiness/target-conditioned summaries, boundary ports and S. `w_i=radius_i^2/sum radius_j^2` uses public physical size; all-zero/invalid size makes S invalid. No child is deleted or frozen. Parent owns a persistent phase Phi; its initial mode is arg(m) when |m|>=1e-6, otherwise zero with a low-coherence flag. Phi persists between ticks rather than being replaced by arg(m).

Proposed bounded parent projection: `dPhi/dt = Omega + F(group_geometry, public_context) + K_up * Im(m * exp(-i Phi))`, with Omega in [-2,2] rad/s, F in [-1,1] rad/s and K_up in [0,2] rad/s. R's per-gun thin command heads consume the same token/context encoder as P plus sin/cos(Phi), m, validity and normalized relative geometry. Parent mode therefore changes aim/timing/shift commands; the unit consumes them, native geometry evolves, direct geometry/modes are republished, and parent forcing/entrainment changes. Parent RK4 uses the same held-input procedure and 5 rad/s increment / 4 s^-1 conservative Jacobian envelope as the unit projection; couple read/commit synchronously, deriving any stronger joint bound before execution. A single parent has no fabricated parent-parent topology.

This loop is a candidate G↔M mechanism. Qualification requires fixed-context geometry→parent-mode and parent-mode→command/realized-geometry probes, plus separately disabled up-mode and down-mode paths. Freeze parent phase, remove K_up, remove geometry forcing only, remove Phi-only command input, remove up-mode summary only, and replace child summaries with matched shuffled summaries (same validity/readiness) as distinct interventions. **Complete mode-to-command removal** additionally zeros Phi features, m/dispersion, every per-gun child-mode feature and any recurrent attention/memory channel carrying prior mode information in the command readout; retain public readiness/geometry and rerun from fight start under the intervention. The Phi-only control leaves direct child-mode→command paths and is explicitly a partial-path probe. Report both controls and actual perturbation sizes. Removing the leader entirely is a utility control, not an isolated directional ablation.

For N2 children, modes are their live learned unit phases. For N1/N1r cross arms, use the first two coordinates of the existing feedforward hidden state/recurrent memory as a bounded summary, unit-normalized with an explicit zero-norm flag. This fixed projection introduces no unfitted auxiliary parameters. P and R see the identical summaries for the chosen unit arm. R-over-N1 is a hybrid with neural child summaries, **not** evidence that resonator units composed into a resonator. N2+R is the source-aligned two-level candidate. No ID-based phase initialization is used in new arms: shared zero initial phase plus observable readiness/forcing diversity, tested under permutation/reflection.

Up/down is lawful: unit owner publishes direct mode/ports; parent owner composes them; parent emits bounded commands on those ports. The upper-facing group publication is `{X,L,M_eff,ports,S,digest}`, not the member list. Parent tactical processing legitimately reads its **direct** gun publications, as decision 0035 requires. Raw gun latent state, engine internals and descendants below the direct-unit boundary belong to owner/evaluator diagnostics and never the upper prediction API. No upper open-loop prediction is qualified here. S invalidates parent commands and routes explicit empty-command fallback; fallback cost/rate is reported.

## 6. J correction and learning procedure

Keep the v2 stored-state/30-tick burn-in/90-tick BPTT procedure symmetrically for N1r/N2 and recurrent leaders, including full epoch refresh, one-epoch possible state staleness, unchanged-weights equivalence and attached neighbor gradients. Float64 state, Adam .001, zero decay, gradient norm cap1, batch4, ten-epoch maximum and finite-stop rules remain unless prospectively amended. Selection is each model's own minimum validation loss, ties earliest; no test/mechanism selection. Record learning curves; do not change LR or checkpoint averaging based on this panel's results.

**J is trainable in stage 1b**, removing its frozen-coordinate mask and zero inherited optimizer moments. Its training signal is an auxiliary normalized **continuous total-goal loss before native clipping**: the legal-softmax expected move-candidate goal + differentiable N2 drift is fitted to the unit oracle's requested categorical goal + shared J=0 baseline drift, with a commanded shift represented in the conditional move label and never added a second time. This expectation is only a differentiable loss surrogate; deployment still chooses a single hard argmax move, never averages opposing safe actions. Report surrogate and hard-decoded goal errors separately. Use mean squared error of goal/100 px, weight1 in every unit arm, identical oracle examples/masks; native trajectory is constant and no physics gradient is claimed. This exposes J to a real differentiable loss rather than pretend categorical CE trains deployment-only drift. Teacher J=0 may drive J to zero; that is an admissible result and cannot support mode→spacing use. J input effects through unit mode/command readouts are diagnosed separately. Initialize J at .01 for trainable N2 (declare its raw logit) so the old unique .5 cohesion prior is absent; A/B/share remain fixed.

Fit a matched **N2-J=0 control** with J identically zero from training through deployment, three seeds and otherwise identical losses/budgets. Also apply post-fit `J0_only` (drift modulation only) and `blind_move_only` (zero mode/field/group channels in movement logits only). Their combination is a separately named `J0_and_blind_move`, not the meaning of either. Old `no_mode_to_geometry` is historical and must not be reused with new semantics. Trainable J vs trained-J0 measures adaptation; post-fit J0 measures reliance, not the same question. K0/frozen-phase/no-reset/topology-only/no-geometry-forcing retain distinct paths and complete definitions; distance/forcing ablation does not remove every action→mode feedback. Numerical and mask assertions must be amended before a run; old `assert_shared_baseline` cannot validate trainable J unchanged.

## 9. Closest methods and the bounded RRG difference

The hierarchy is related to commander/unit micro-control and manager/worker policies. [FeUdal Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.01161) separates abstract manager goals from worker actions at different temporal resolutions; [Feudal Multi-Agent Hierarchies](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.08492) applies managerial goals to cooperating workers. Stage1b initially uses the same decision cadence at both levels and imitation, so it is not a replication of their hierarchical RL objectives.

[CommNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.07736) learns continuous communication with agent policies; [BiCNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.10069) uses bidirectional recurrent coordination for StarCraft combat; [TarMAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.11187) learns targeted communication. These are relevant strong conventional designs, not claimed inferior methods. The set-attention leader is a conventional comparator, and a recurrent message unit N1r controls for generic communication/memory.

The proposed RRG-specific addition is a persistent level-2 mode **composed and driven by live direct-unit modes**, with geometry affecting mode evolution and mode affecting bounded downward commands that change subsequent geometry. Hierarchy, attention or messaging alone is not the claimed difference. Causal path interventions, an exercised drill and useful paired task improvement are separate requirements; no architectural naming establishes them.
