## Findings and concrete fixes

### R2-F1 — High: the revised observation schema exceeds its boundary and still lacks a decodable tensor contract

**Evidence:** R2.4:145–146 gives friends and enemies the same self fields, including energy and preparation/time-to-release. Adapter `react.h:13–20` and `react.cpp:28–34` put energy, prep, windup and time rate in own-only records; enemy preparation is exposed only through casts from living enemy guns already winding up at a living friendly target. README:59–70 explicitly excludes enemy energy. Enemy cooldown **is** in the existing public ObservedUnit (`react.cpp:30`); flagging cooldown itself as a leak would be incorrect.

R2.4 mentions a normalization ledger but supplies no scale constants, feature widths, version identifier or field masks. It retains nearest-12 target support and the original threat cap without a threat-ranking/overflow rule. A public planner can select an aim outside the bounded target-relative head or a target absent from those 12 slots. Shots still omit birth/age, which the copied reaction uses for its 0.12 s latency (`react.cpp:49`). Field-token geometry/timing is not specified. Self time rate, cast cost and weapon flight/blast quantities needed for timing/legality are not fully defined.

**Fix:** supply one field-by-field allowlist and tensor/normalization table, with separate own and enemy masks. Restrict enemy prep to the existing cast tokens; retain lawful public cooldown. Define side mirroring, time origin, units/scales, padding, sorting and threat overflow. Declare target-none versus unavailable target, out-of-support counts and aim/move bounds. Constrain the teacher to the same candidate/action support or deliberately enlarge that support from public inputs; never silently convert an unavailable target to none. Treat non-finite outputs as failures, separately from ordinary finite range/participation projection. Add a boundary-leak stop row and later fixtures that show changing tactic ID, enemy energy, private skills and RNG cannot change tensors or policy output.

### R2-F2 — High: six-tick caching does not yet specify a lawful cast state machine

**Evidence:** R2.3:137–139 makes Hold block only new windups and makes an existing cast always release, with a one-interval maximum hold. Existing adapter Hold blocks both start and release while windup advances (`react.cpp:163–167`, README:74–77). Native `gamePrep` requires living target, reach, cooldown, energy and cast permission before starting, and starts cooldown/energy consumption then (`src/native/combat_rules.cpp:58–68`). `actNovice` requires a target, evaluates gun target reach before walking, and can consume a prepared cast without launching if the target has moved out of range (`combat.cpp:9–34`). Explicit-aim reach is a separate generated gate. Projecting the aim to a nearest legal point cannot repair a missing/dead/out-of-range target or a body block.

The observation is after time/cooldown/reflex updates, before controller preparation and before `gamePrep`, not simply the beginning of a physics tick (`combat.cpp:82–125`; adapter `build.py:26–31`). The first policy tick, action-cache expiry, interrupted/replaced aim during windup, phase-window evaluation cadence and acknowledgement/reset ordering are not fixed. Reading phase at 30 Hz while N1 outputs fire only at 5 Hz would give N2 an extra fast action channel despite the shared-cache promise.

**Fix:** give a transition table for idle/cooldown, start-ready, winding, prepared, blocked, released and consumed-without-launch. Separate request to start from permission to release; explicitly identify any new automatic-release behavior. Define native-legality precedence over the hold bound, cancellation/retarget rules and which aim is locked to a pending cast. Fix first tick, six-tick expiry and exact snapshot point. Choose a common 5 Hz fire decision or expose an equivalent declared fast head to both networks. Specify phase-window/crossing latching, cooldown-to-phase relation and reset on actual launch, not merely permission. Later fixtures must cover target death, min/max range, walking across reach, cooldown expiry, Hold during prep, body blocking, delayed release and a missed phase window. Add a timing-matched constrained-teacher reference; keep the 30 Hz teacher explicitly separate.

### R2-F3 — High: the constrained teacher and its labels still cannot be queried as specified

R2.5:163 combines post-arbitration intent with actual release/aim without choosing how a 30 Hz teacher becomes one six-tick training action. A later release is observable to the collector but is not a same-tick start-permission label; its absence during cooldown is not Hold.

Its geometry hook runs after `gamePrep`; its fixtures prove declared synthetic projection parity, not full-elite behavior. Calling the existing planner at the pre-policy shadow point may produce no new labels for a cast becoming ready later that tick. Copying RNG/state alone does not define the query semantics, especially queues, teacher history and branch-light candidate selection.

**Fix:** name the entire constrained teacher: ordinary gun target and movement selection, cast-start decision, REACT precedence, participation, aim planner, timing and exact options. Reuse v6's named public projection/defaults and copied output collector rather than calling `artilleryVolley` on the living student World. For the first slice, exclude delayed/coming-gun schedules as v6 does, or extend the student action contract to represent them. Supply a causal label join table: decision-time target/move/start permission; pending-cast aim identity; actual launch/consumption acknowledgements; diagnostic outcomes. Mask undefined losses and preserve joint volley identity. Choose a teacher under the same six-tick cache for fitting, or explicitly define and measure the resampling approximation. Same-state DAgger must query this policy without advancing the living world; any scratch preparation/readiness calculation, persistent teacher state and branch flags need an exact declared meaning. Shadow isolation and action/launch parity are separate required fixtures. Full-elite internal plans, search scores, doctrine IDs and rollout predictions remain excluded from student tensors.

### R2-F4 — High: N2 imitation still has undefined gradient and recurrent-state paths

**Evidence:** R2.6:175 uses a hard phase-in-window permit, while §6:71 retains cross-entropy for fire. No differentiable fire logit or surrogate is supplied. R2.6:174 also learns radius, while topology is hard and treated as no-gradient within a step (:181). If radius only enters a detached inclusion mask, ordinary BPTT supplies no radius-learning path. Three-second windows still lack initialization, burn-in, detach, identity/reset and joint-state rules. A 5 Hz feature/label dataset supplies only 15 observations in 3 s; the stated 90-tick geometry-dependent integration requires the intervening positions/inputs or a declared approximation.

**Fix:** specify a bounded differentiable phase-based fire logit and its exact deterministic native decoder/threshold/latch, or a named surrogate with separately tested approximation. Fix radius during imitation and reserve it for derivative-free search, or supply a differentiable distance gate with an audited runtime counterpart. State which parameters each loss can reach. Keep all live guns together, integrate joint neighbor state synchronously, detach only at declared truncation boundaries and preserve unit IDs across slot changes/deaths. Define fight/series resets, initial phases and history reconstruction/burn-in. Store a joint 30 Hz geometry stream with a declared forcing cadence, or explicitly label interpolation/sample-hold as a different training model and quantify parity error. Offline BPTT through recorded geometry does not differentiate through native motion, target selection or collision outcomes. Future checks need finite gradients and finite-difference checks away from switches, joint sequence Python/C++ parity and long sparse-roster rollouts. These requirements follow the differentiability limitations documented in [PyTorch autograd mechanics](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/notes/autograd.html#gradients-for-non-differentiable-functions); they do not imply that BPTT is impossible.

### R2-F5 — High: v6's numerical bounds do not apply to the revised phase/C4 motion law

**Evidence:** R2.6:177–182 says bounds and substeps match `s4_v6_complex.cpp`. That integrator bounds complex cubic dynamics with μ/K/Kt/ω/pressure checks, a cubic-amplitude envelope and its own Lipschitz expression (`src/native/s4_v6_complex.cpp:9–14,61–77`). R2 now uses real phase plus C4's inverse-distance movement. Those amplitude constants/proofs do not bound this different equation. C4 explicitly regularizes distance by eps (`geomind/c4_model.py:85–101`); R2's equation omits eps and its ledger does not give the length/time scale. Using `exp(-r²)` directly on battlefield pixel distances, for example r=100 px, would effectively eliminate coupling. This is a unit-conversion counterexample, not an assertion that the future implementation will do that.

**Fix:** define dimensionless r=d/L0, radius in the same coordinates, eps/minimum distance, and conversion of C4 motion to bounded px/s or a goal contribution. Specify A/B/J/K/ω/forcing and movement-share bounds. Keep actual engine positions authoritative: declare whether geometry is frozen through phase RK4 or supplied by a defined interpolant; do not integrate fictitious C4 positions and substitute them for collision-resolved motion. Derive the revised phase-rate/Jacobian and repulsion bounds, choose dt/substeps and cap, and reuse v6's monitoring/retry/atomic-failure architecture with valid new bounds. Gradient clipping does not establish forward stability. Dropping a failed training window must be counted with a stop threshold and no partial optimizer update; inference numerical failure must invalidate a clean engineering receipt. No runs here establish stiffness, stability or CPU infeasibility. Stuart–Landau has been superseded for this slice, so its stiffness is no longer the operative acceptance question.

### R2-F6 — High: the new ablations do not isolate the claimed RRG loop

**Evidence:** R2.7:189 calls topology freezing removal of geometry→mode. Actual C4 distinguishes that partial intervention from complete removal: `ABLATIONS["no_geometry_to_mode"]` sets both distance_weighted=False and frozen_phase_topology=True (`geomind/c4_model.py:13–16,42–48`). Frozen topology still recalculates current r and `exp(-r*r)` (:92–101). C5 reuses both channels and matched geometry/phase perturbations (`c5_experiment.py:11–17,216–242`). R2 also injects geometry-bearing observation forcing g·φ(o) into phase; that is another possible G→M path. J=0 removes the explicit C4 motion term's mode dependence, but a learned movement head that reads phase/field would retain M→G. The draft does not settle that head's input.

**Fix:** reuse C4's complete distance-blind plus fixed-phase-topology intervention, naming topology-only separately. Either hold geometry-derived forcing fixed in a causal probe or explicitly scope the tested channel to coupling. Audit every phase/field input into movement; make the complete M→G ablation phase-blind while preserving observation-only movement, normalization and decoder. Use matched geometric and phase interventions and report response of phase/motion, not only fight-score harm. K=0 task degradation is useful evidence of coupling reliance, not a complete causal closure test.

The local loop is a plausible **geometry-coupled oscillatory policy projection**, conditional on these repairs and measurements. It is not yet an accepted C4 resonator or a C5 effective group. C5 detector criteria alone are also insufficient for a C5 hierarchy claim: reuse original-member recovery/direct-part validity, published state/ports, size-weighted natural rates, matched transfer and open-loop coarse/full prediction with error/reopening boundaries if that later claim is made. Keep H-U/H-COMP/H-BG/H-PS/H-RBG NOT_TESTED in the slice. R5 allows new effective equations; reverting to literal C4 physics is not required, and frozen code must remain untouched.

### R2-F7 — High: imitation losses and DAgger aggregation remain unsafe or undefined

**Evidence:** R2 does not replace the original Gaussian move/aim heads or NLL (:43,71), or specify deterministic versus sampled deployment. Two safe dodge labels on opposite sides of a blast can average into its centre. Same-state labels/aggregation are necessary, but R2.5 gives no rounds, query cap, mixture, balanced shared data union, head weights or checkpoint selection. N2 needs joint chronological samples; independent flattened gun rows destroy that contract. These are the same compounding-error/data-distribution concerns addressed by the [original DAgger paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686).

**Fix:** use a bounded candidate-direction head or a small mixture for movement with an explicit safe mode-selection decoder; define aim multimodality likewise where joint assignments differ. Do not deploy a conditional Gaussian mean as an unspecified dodge policy. Define active-fire/readiness/move/aim masks, variance floors, per-head weights and imbalance handling. Seal whole-fight/series splits before windowing; never refit on validation/reporting looks. Declare a small bounded DAgger schedule with shared balanced union of N1/N2 (and N1r if used) visited-state labels, recovery and one/two-gun coverage, teacher mixing if any and validation-only checkpoint selection. Data aggregation/refitting cannot reconstruct omitted public features or actions outside decoder support.

Size joint streams once per tick instead of duplicating them for every unit; state average versus hard upper-bound duration, orientation count, sequence batch, epochs, optimizer state and disk/peak-memory allowance. Project collection/public teacher/shadow, each fit, DAgger, export, ES and reporting before execution. A later authorized bounded timing sample must use these actual paths. For a whole-system equal-training-budget result, fix counts and report core-seconds, memory, inference latency, teacher queries, RHS evaluations/substeps and search overhead; do not call those counts equal compute. For oscillator attribution, N1r needs identical local graph/access, previous-target grouping, persistent identity, memory/update cadence and decoder. Define self-exclusion, empty/low-resultant group fallback and simultaneous previous-assignment reads. Declare bounded ES subset dimensions per arm. A third control can be deferred from the first executable slice if oscillator-specific attribution is also deferred.

**Fix:** name this a drill-training surrogate, with a declared non-passivity/task-completion eligibility rule and raw deaths/kills/time visible; keep the owner's no-loss-budget priority. Define separable-CMA rank construction, null/tie handling, uncertainty and incumbent retention, learnable dimension/bounds and PFSP prior/exponent/floor/fallback (or explicitly use uniform slice sampling). Use common immutable candidate draws and an independent draw RNG. Count physical fights, not ambiguous “pairs”: 3,200 per arm, 6,400 for N1+N2, or 9,600 including N1r, before baselines, repeats and fresh reporting. Freeze total caps for those additions. Decision 0033 applies to post-selection outcome readings at 50/100/200, with fresh reporting seeds; never repeatedly select on those looks. Validate any later drill-surrogate/series association before treating it as a series objective. Stage 1 can be a deliverable without this search.
