## Findings and required fixes

### F1 — High: using the current adapter preserves a scripted policy inside the network arm

**Evidence:** draft §§3–4 requires learned dodging with no scripted dodge. `s4_react_adapter_v1/react.cpp:89–105` calls S4V7Controller, computes copied REACT and selects it before submitted commands. `submit():122–137` cannot override an existing REACT/body/failure winner. `react.h:43` declares this controller final. The v6 lab overlays further script atoms rather than providing a neutral network host.

**Consequence:** placing network outputs into `submit()` produces a network plus scripted REACT arm. Its survival gain could come from the copied rule, and calling it a fully learned policy would be false. A named neutral factory/host and observation/export seam are still needed.

**Fix:** add new files that reuse the immutable observation and command legality mechanics, with a neutral network controller that does not first invoke P16, v7, copied REACT, E1, R1 or the volley planner. Preserve native physics, body reflexes and abilities policy equally across arms. Separate mandatory legality/participation projection from tactical candidate selection; record raw policy intent, constrained intent, reason and actual execution. Label any mixed-role scaffold explicitly. Keep existing adapter/lab binaries and sealed declarations unchanged.

### F2 — High: 5 Hz actions have no defined meaning across the 30 Hz cast lifecycle

**Evidence:** native `config.h:8` defaults to 1/30 s; `combat.cpp:82–125` prepares/controllers/decides every tick, then applies cast gates, advances preparation and runs the artillery planner. Adapter snapshots clear command records each tick (`react.h:55`). Hold suppresses new preparation and release, while an existing preparation keeps advancing (`react.cpp:158–167`; `combat_rules.cpp:58–69`). Cooldown starts when preparation starts, not after release. Release grants permission; it does not launch immediately. Native launches also depend on live target, range, lane/body conditions and post-move checks. Draft §§3,4,6 neither supplies a six-tick cache nor resolves phase crossing versus fire permission.

**Consequence:** a short release pulse can start a windup and then hold the completed cast; a decoder that waits for “prepared” before releasing can prevent preparation from ever starting. A phase event between 5 Hz samples can be lost. Learned reaction sees up to one decision interval of additional delay against a teacher deciding each tick.

**Fix:** specify the exact observation point, first decision tick and six-tick action duration. Cache absolute goal/aim and stable target identity, not offsets reapplied to a moving unit every tick. Define target death/slot churn, movement stop/multiplier, cast-start permission, pending cast, completed-cast hold bound, actual-launch acknowledgement and reset/cancellation. Distinguish start-ready, winding and release-ready masks. State when phase evolves, when crossings latch, and when phase resets after an actual launch. Continue native legality checks at every tick; never bypass cooldown/windup. Treat neural reaction latency as a measured limitation, or give both networks the same declared fast reaction head. Future fixtures must cover cooldown expiry, target death during windup, moving out of aim reach, Hold during an existing cast, missed crossings and variable branch dt. A timing-matched teacher arm is needed before interpreting imitation fidelity.

### F3 — High: the observation/action encoding loses information required for labels and legality

**Evidence:** draft §3 has role/HP/cooldown/readiness but no explicit preparation, time rate, energy, range/minimum range, body size, movement speed, arena inset or windup fields. The adapter already exposes many of these through ObservedUnit and OwnView. Its ShotView includes direction, birth time, speed and remaining distance; reducing every threat to landing point/time/radius/inside loses aimed-shot path and reaction-age information. “8 most relevant” has no sorting/tie/overflow rule. Nearest-12 enemy slots can exclude the elite's artillery target or a useful distant enemy gun. Current/other-unit targets can point outside that list.

**Fix:** provide a versioned tensor schema, normalization ledger and decoder: units in px/s/game-seconds versus scaled features, bounds, masks, deterministic distance/ID ties, orientation transform and inverse transform for actions. Include self cast state and weapon/body fields required by the decoder; encode shot, shell, field and cast threat types with their distinct geometry/timing. Define threat ranking and overflow diagnostics. Map persistent IDs to transient pointer slots and define none versus outside-support separately. Either enlarge/construct candidate sets from the same legal public information, or explicitly label a constrained teacher and report lost-label/target coverage. Never relabel an unavailable teacher target as “none.” Give move/aim projection and invalid-numeric behavior a declared contract. Failures must be counted and disqualify a clean engineering receipt; silent hold fallback is not evidence of numerical health.

### F4 — High: a native decision trace is not the elite's complete executed-action label

**Evidence:** draft §5 requests target, move goal, release, aim and dodge every 0.2 s. Native `decisionTrace` is captured after `decideUnit`, before fire gates, `gamePrep`, `artilleryVolley` and `actNovice` (`combat.cpp:117–133`). Artillery launches/queues joint PlannedShots later, including points not coincident with the chosen unit target (`artillery.cpp:38–41,51–99`). Actual aim may be resolved at the launch call site. `bcData` in `search.cpp` labels commander plan choice, not unit action. Adapter `executed()` means post-bridge intent, not an actual shot; `teacherReact` contains only activation/goal of the primitive.

**Fix:** design a new native collector with explicit timestamps and schema for pre-policy public snapshot, teacher candidate movement (including move flag/stop/multiplier), post-arbitration intent, cast start/hold/continue, scheduled joint volley, and actual release/aim/target. Join them by fight/tick/unit ID and volley identity; keep launch outcomes separate from action labels. Select a 5 Hz label policy explicitly: resampled instantaneous intent, six-tick teacher continuation, or teacher run under the student's timing restrictions. Quantify labels the student's decoder cannot reproduce (long goals, omitted targets, native DodgeFire, delayed shots). Train fire permission from the correct event, not from absence of a launch while cooldown is active. Mask undefined aim/move/fire losses and preserve joint battery labels. Keep full elite as a reference if a constrained teacher is necessary; do not silently equate them.

The full elite's internal search may use actual enemy profile/hidden state and cloned continuations. This may create a privileged teacher, which can be labelled as such, but no internal candidate scores, plan IDs, tactic IDs, RNG, predicted future trajectories or hidden enemy state may enter the student tensors. The v6 public planner projection, with rollout disabled and neutral enemy defaults, is a useful bounded teacher option, not equivalent to full elite.

### F5 — High: the existing shadow cannot supply full-elite DAgger labels

**Evidence:** `react.cpp:149–156` deliberately builds an unformed teacher with only the dodge trio, resets tactical state and calls decideUnit without commander/planner/search. Its comments and README explicitly say primitive REACT, not T-elite. `World::copyAuthorityFrom` resets branch thinking; `search.cpp:26` suppresses branch lookahead and `artillery.cpp:61` uses a lighter branch planner. Separate RNG alone does not restore full teacher behavior or state.

**Fix:** specify a full teacher oracle on student-visited joint states, including pack/search history, teacher memory initialization/advancement, branch-thinking flags, bounded nested search and the exact point in tick order where labels are queried. Do not advance physical time merely to obtain a same-state label. State what “elite on this student state” means when its formation history does not exist. Test clone isolation and byte-identical student trajectory with shadow on/off; continuation parity or a declared reduced-teacher scope is required. The existing primitive shadow may be reused only for its named REACT labels.

Define DAgger rounds, query/sample caps, aggregation weights, checkpoint selection, teacher mixing if any and recovery/sparse-roster coverage. Both networks must train on the same balanced union of N1-visited and N2-visited labelled trajectories, or explicitly relinquish “same data.” Split whole trajectories before windowing; group series together and exclude validation/test/reporting seeds from refits. Keep sequence order and all coupled own units together for N2. Aggregation/refitting is essential, but DAgger cannot recover information omitted from the student's observation. This distinction follows the [original DAgger paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686).

### F6 — High: N2 misidentifies C4/C5 and its ablations cannot establish an RRG resonator

**Evidence:** draft §4 calls Stuart–Landau “as in v6 and C4/C5,” then equates units in phase with C5 formation. `geomind/c4_model.py:82–113` uses coupled positions and phases: phase-dependent motion and distance/topology-dependent phase rates with normalized local neighbors and RK4. `c5_compose.py:1–19` integrates that unchanged C4 element law on every live member. `c5_detect.py` requires membership, geometric stability, mode lock/signature, matched perturbation recovery and live direct parts. C5 state publication, coarse/full response and causal probes are more than synchronization. The tactical v6 complex oscillator is a different projection.

**Fix:** correct the model ancestry and declare N2's tested subset. Stuart–Landau is allowed as a new projection; R5 permits different effective equations and does not require literal physical-force equations. A learned mode→movement→actual battlefield geometry→mode loop can qualify, but must be causally tested. Reuse C4's stable local-neighbor selection/degree normalization, frozen topology within integration stages, circular/unwrapped phase conventions, complete G→M and M→G interventions and matched controls. Reuse C5's detector/recovery and published-state recipes only if claiming effective groups; preserve direct-part validity, boundary ports, size/rate normalization, open-loop coarse/full comparison and error/reopening boundaries for any later hierarchy. Do this read-only or through new files; never modify frozen C4/C5.

For the first milestone, call N2 a geometry-coupled oscillatory policy. Report task usefulness separately from H-M; leave H-U, H-COMP, H-BG/H-PS/H-RBG and higher-state prediction NOT_TESTED unless their protocols are supplied. K=0 damage can show useful coupling; frozen-z damage can show useful memory. Neither establishes effective-unit formation or source recursion. Task usefulness and scientific source qualification are distinct gates.

### F7 — High: BPTT lacks a stable numerical, state and differentiable-readout contract

**Evidence:** draft §4's unnormalized sum and learned K/input forcing have no degree/rate/amplitude bounds, solver, step size or failure policy. Three-second unrolling (§6) defines neither initialization nor how neighbor-state gradients are propagated. Tactical `src/native/s4_v6_complex.cpp:9–14,41–99` already supplies bounded rates/forcing, normalized coupling, held topology, RK4 stage monitoring, derived amplitude/Lipschitz bounds, capped substeps, retry and atomic failure. These bounds do not automatically cover the draft's new equation. A hard phase crossing cannot directly train the stated Bernoulli cross-entropy head.

**Fix:** constrain μ, ω, coupling row sums and input forcing prospectively; normalize distances and coupling, and derive an amplitude/Jacobian envelope for the selected law. Choose identical training/native integration, physical dt and substep policy. Use real/imaginary state for a straightforward CPU export. Declare frozen joint graph/state reads, topology-change gradient treatment, recurrent identity across slot changes/deaths, initialization, reset between fights/series, sequence burn-in, truncated-BPTT detach and gradient clipping. Never initialize every training window to zero while deployment retains history. Backpropagate through joint own-unit dynamics, not independent per-unit samples with unexplained detached neighbors. Recorded geometry yields recurrent-state training conditional on teacher trajectories; it does not differentiate through native physics, target selection or collision outcomes.

Provide a differentiable fire logit/phase surrogate and its exact relationship to runtime crossing/readiness; report any surrogate-gradient approximation. Reuse v6's safety architecture with a new valid envelope, not its constants unexamined. Required later engineering checks: step refinement, bounded worst cases, finite gradients, surrogate finite-difference checks away from discontinuities, Python/C++ state/output parity, long-horizon rollout and sparse/disconnected/zero-amplitude states. Small step size and dissipative continuous dynamics alone do not prove discrete forward or gradient stability. Conversely, static inspection does not prove Stuart–Landau is too stiff or CPU-infeasible. [coRNN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00951) is a discretized second-order controlled oscillator architecture; its stability results do not transfer automatically to this first-order forced cubic system.

### F10 — Medium: “200 fights” and “CPU only” do not size the dataset or training

**Evidence:** draft §§5–6 gives no fight duration/alive-count model, tensor width, serialization, sequence batch, epochs, thread/worker allocation or storage estimate. Elite collection includes commander and artillery search; its cost is not the lightweight base throughput. “Both orientations” is ambiguous about whether 200 is total or per orientation. An ignored virtualenv is not a reproducible ML dependency pin.

**Fix:** before collection, compute rows as `sum_fights sum_decisions alive_own_units`, not number of fights alone. At 200 total fights, 150 s, 5 Hz and at most 50 own units, the upper bound is 7.5 million unit-decision rows; 200 per orientation doubles it. At an illustrative 400 float32 features, 7.5 million rows consume 12 GB before labels/metadata/activations; an illustrative mean 50 s and 24 live own units gives 1.2 million rows and 1.92 GB. These are sizing examples, not measured duration or a fixed feature schema. Store each joint snapshot once and derive entity features; avoid duplicating all neighbor sets in JSON. Report role/activity/target-support counts: millions of correlated inactive ticks are not millions of independent fights.

Project teacher search + collection/shadow + feature packing + N1 fit + N2 joint BPTT + DAgger + export + ES + reporting, including memory and disk. Three seconds is 15 policy decisions but 90 physical ticks at 30 Hz, multiplied by solver substeps/RK4 evaluations and network/graph work. A later owner-authorized timing sample must use this actual path, not ordinary fight throughput, and must not run now. Use a separate checked-in ML requirements/lock and Python version, ignored environment, training/source/dataset/model hashes, deterministic export schema and dependency/license records. Preserve frozen project pyproject.toml/uv.lock. CPU feasibility remains unverified until bounded projection/calibration; installation alone does not resolve it.

### F11 — Medium: readings, stop rows and ablations are not yet decidable

**Evidence:** draft §§7–8 says “reaches,” “not below,” “at or above,” “clearly hurt,” “about the majority baseline” and “far below” without estimands/margins or denominators. Majority accuracy is inappropriate for continuous move/aim and can be misleading for mostly-hold fire. K=0 and z-freeze can induce distribution shift; no harm does not prove the state is unused (redundancy can preserve outcomes), and harm does not establish a specific RRG mechanism. The baseline condition “V2 if it passes” is not sealed, and different shared timing selections would confound the comparison.

**Fix:** declare development comparison margins/effect sizes and paired uncertainty/read rules before reading results; do not claim equivalence from lack of significance. Reuse the v6 lab structure for mechanism→C3 50/100/200→survivor-only series, immutable arms/binary/spec/teacher/model/dataset identities, separate entropy/draw streams, all-tactic cells (including missing cells), all-fight denominators, won/failed/timeout split, arm-specific healed survivors and first-non-win stop. Freeze scripted baseline and common timing after their actual readings. Ten series are descriptive and cannot certify narrow noninferiority.

Measure legal-target coverage, active-fire precision/recall and launch opportunity loss, movement/aim errors on defined labels, participation, damage/shot and shell, actual deaths, failure rate and role/sparse-roster cells. Symmetric safe move alternatives produce multimodal labels: Gaussian mean inference can move between two safe escapes into the blast. Choose a bounded mixture/candidate-direction head or a declared alternative-label rule, and specify deterministic versus sampled deployment. Loss weighting must balance inactive/active and per-role data; use a variance floor and action masks.

Predeclare acute and, where affordable, retrained ablations: no coupling, broken geometry-to-mode via distance/topology controls, mode-to-action/geometry cut and temporal-state control; add the matched recurrent non-oscillatory comparison if attributing an advantage specifically to oscillation. Preserve forcing/normalization/decoder across arms and report what each ablation removes. Coupling/state ablation degradation establishes task reliance within scope; full RRG claims need F6's interventions. Keep deaths/damage tradeoffs visible under 0034 and compare to the fixed scripted combiner before claiming a resonant combiner. Rewrite each stop row as a measurable yes/no condition, one action and one responsible role; unresolved projections, parity, numerical failures, label-support loss and boundary leaks need stop rows too.
