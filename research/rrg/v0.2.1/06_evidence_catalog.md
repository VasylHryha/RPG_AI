# 06 — Evidence catalogue and open connections

**RRG v0.2.1 · checked 2 October 2026 · living publication companion**

RRG proposes that persistent geometry–mode organization can change the background in which further organization becomes possible. This catalogue explains the real observations and limited mathematical results that make parts of that idea worth considering. It does not require a universal proof before the hypothesis is shared.

**The key reading rule:** an established component is evidence that a mechanism can occur under the reported conditions. Its interpretation as one stage of RRG is a separate claim. Different examples do not automatically combine into one demonstrated recursive chain.

## Two labels, not one evidence ladder

**What kind of result?** Experiment; simulation; theoretical/mathematical derivation. A study can contain more than one. A review is an orientation source rather than an extra experiment. Internal RRG calculations are separately labelled and not counted as independent support.

**How does it relate?** A *component* demonstrates a local operation; a *partial bridge* connects operations in one system; an *analogy* shares organizational logic without demonstrating the same microscopic process; a *constraint/comparator* identifies limits or an alternative mechanism. None of these labels is a confidence score for all of RRG.

## Claim map

| Claim | Limited operation being discussed |
|---|---|
| C0 | Fluctuations or a uniform starting regime can participate in pattern formation or interaction. |
| C1 | Geometry and mode structure constrain or affect one another. |
| C2 | Coupled oscillators can develop collective phase/frequency relationships. |
| C3 | A driven field or vibration can reorganize material. |
| C4 | An organization, boundary or interaction changes what its surroundings can do. |
| C5 | A nonliving pattern or chemical organization can propagate or multiply. |
| C6 | Lower-level dynamics admit useful collective variables and an effective background description. |
| C7 | Controlled conditions can implement selected effective dynamics. |

These are not eight already-proved stages in the history of the universe. C0, for example, distinguishes noise-induced ordering from deterministic instability. C6 distinguishes a physical new environment from our mathematical description of it.

## Start with these connections

For the source-direction intuition, begin with **E19** (random-field-mediated interaction) and **E04** (noise-assisted pattern formation in a model). For reciprocal organization, use **E07/E08/E18**. For multiplication without life, use **E10/E11/E20**. For the next-level background, read **E14/E21** together with their limits. E17 and E22 prevent the catalogue from turning all organization into evidence for stronger resonance.

## E01 — Experimental Study of the Triplet Synchronization of Coupled Nonidentical Mechanical Metronomes

**experiment · component / constraint · claims C2.**

**Reported result:** Coupled nonidentical metronomes exhibit a collective triplet synchronization relation even where individual pairs are asynchronous.

**Already supplied:** Metronomes, their energy supply, and mechanical coupling already exist.

**Limit of the RRG connection:** Collective coordination need not mean every oscillator has one identical frequency or phase. This does not demonstrate the creation of the oscillators.

**Source:** Jia, Song, Liu, Kurths & Xiao (2015), *Scientific Reports 5, 17008*. [DOI: 10.1038/srep17008](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep17008).

**Verification coverage:** Publisher HTML, including experimental description. Checked 2026-10-02.

## E02 — Controlling the motion of multiple objects on a Chladni plate

**experiment · component · claims C1, C3.**

**Reported result:** Plate vibrations can transport particles; the study uses controlled sequences of excitation to manipulate multiple objects.

**Already supplied:** A manufactured plate, particles, actuator, camera and control algorithm are provided.

**Limit of the RRG connection:** Distinguish ordinary nodal accumulation from the paper’s actively controlled manipulation. Neither establishes autonomous particle-to-field feedback or reproduction.

**Source:** Zhou, Sariola, Latifi & Liimatainen (2016), *Nature Communications 7, 12764*. [DOI: 10.1038/ncomms12764](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms12764).

**Verification coverage:** Publisher HTML and author-institution abstract. Supplementary videos linked on the paper page were not watched. Checked 2026-10-02.

## What the catalogue does not yet supply

There is no documented complete example in this release of unstructured activity producing a resonator, that resonator causally creating another distinct persistent level, and the process continuing indefinitely under one unchanged microscopic rule. This is a limit of the collected evidence, not a claim that no such system can exist or a reason to stop publishing the hypothesis.

The highest-value additions would connect adjacent operations in the **same system**. Useful observations would compare an environment before and after an emergent structure forms, identify how its response changes, and show what new organization becomes stable. Negative cases—termination, destruction, coexistence and shrinking scales—are informative too.

No source count is a probability of truth. Papers sharing a model, apparatus, authors or data are not automatically independent confirmations. Ordinary explanations of these experiments remain valid competitors; calling them RRG-compatible does not make them RRG-specific evidence.

## Videos, data and reuse

A video can make a reported effect understandable but is not a substitute for identifying the apparatus, conditions and original paper. Supplementary material was not watched or reproduced in this audit. Link to original paper pages rather than presenting third-party compilations as evidence. Do not copy figures or movies into the public website without checking their licences; a citation is not a reuse licence.

## Adding or changing a case

Record the claim ID, original source, result type, actual finding, supplied conditions, RRG interpretation, important alternative explanation, limit, publication date and what was inspected. Mark preprints as preprints; do not count a preprint and its journal version twice. Check the publisher’s correction/retraction notices when visible, and record any unresolved check. This release is not a complete retraction-database or raw-data audit.

Keep a prior entry when revising it and state the reason in the changelog. Updates happen when the project is reviewed; no automatic monitoring is running. A failed search is an evidence gap, not proof of absence.

**Publication position:** present the proposed common connection confidently as a hypothesis, give established examples their proper scope, and invite explanations, objections, additional cases and optional derivations.
